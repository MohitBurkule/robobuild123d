# Vector: reference description

Photo: `reference/vector.aws71.webp`, front-right three-quarter view from above, background removed. The front faces lower right.

## Overall form
- A gold or amber translucent printed chassis (sparkly PETG-like) with white printed inner parts.
- Plan is about 100 x 90 mm and the body is low (about 30 mm). Two long forward "legs" or forks on the front make a U-shaped front opening.

## Chassis
- Rear: a flat gold top deck carries "VECTOR" embossed or printed in dark letters, reading from the right side across the rear.
- A black rectangular inset panel (about 30 x 20 mm, a battery cover or grip pad) sits in the middle of the deck.
- Small silver screws sit at the deck corners (4 visible).
- Front: two gold side rails run forward, one each side. They are triangular in side view: tall at the rear (about 30 mm) and tapering to sharp points at the floor at the front, forming two wedge forks or skids.
- The white inner side walls show inside the rails. The rails stand about 0.35 of the length ahead of the deck.

## Weapon (lifter / arm, axis x)
- Between the front rails sits a black arm. A large black disc or pulley hub (about 35 mm diameter) is set on a horizontal axis across the robot (axis x) at the front of the deck.
- From the hub, a short thick black arm reaches forward and down. At its end a crossbar carries three square silver blocks (about 14 mm cubes, each with a black socket-head screw at its centre), spanning about 0.5 of the width.
- The arm swings up and over: it is a lifter or hammer.
- Two tall triangular silver aluminium plates stand up from the white inner walls either side of the hub, about 25 mm above the deck, as arm stops or srimech uprights. Each is fixed with 2-3 screws near its lower end.

## Wheels
- Two wheels at the rear corners, outside the gold body: black rubber tyres with chunky tread on gold printed hubs.
- Diameter about 0.35 of the length. The wheels stand slightly above the deck.
- A gold wheel guard or ring partly surrounds the left wheel, as a hub cap.

## Colour summary
- Gold or amber: the main body, rails and hubs.
- White: the inner walls and front bulkhead.
- Black: the arm, hub, tyres and deck pad.
- Silver: the blocks, uprights and screws.
