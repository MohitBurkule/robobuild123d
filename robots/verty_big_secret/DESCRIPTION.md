# Verty Big Secret: what the reference photos show

Photos: `reference/verty_big_secret.aws72.webp` (front-right iso, two-tooth disc), `aws74.webp` (front-right, low, multi-tooth disc), `aws76.webp` (front-left, high, single big hook disc). The disc changes from event to event. The chassis, forks, plates and wheels are the same in all three. Axes: +y forward, +x right, z up.

## Overall layout
- **Twin pods with a centre slot.** Two separate rectangular pods sit either side of an open centre slot. The slot is about as wide as one pod (roughly 1/3 of the total width) and is open at the front, top and bottom. The pods are joined only at the rear: a bridge or cross tube behind the slot. aws72 shows a round black cross tube high up at the rear of the slot.
- **Proportions.** Each pod is about 3.5 to 4 times as long as it is tall. The pod height is about 0.7 to 0.8 of the wheel diameter. The whole body without forks is about 1.1 to 1.2 times as long as it is wide.
- **Colour.** Black/dark-green PETG/PLA everywhere, with a visible layer texture. Deck plates are slightly glossier black (carbon or PC).
- **Accents.**
  - The weapon motor is a red-anodised outrunner (FLASH HOBBY) in aws72/74 and a black one in aws76.
  - The steel disc edges are ground bright: green/white reflections on the edges.
  - There are silver nyloc nuts and bolt heads, and a red JST plug lead on top of the right pod (aws72).

## Pods
- **Shape.** Each pod is a box with vertical sides and a flat top deck.
- **Deck plates.** A thin flat plate (about 1.5 to 2 mm, black, slightly glossy) covers each pod top for its full length. It has 4 black button-head screws, one near each corner, with washers.
  - The plate overhangs a little and has a step/notch where it meets the side plate.
  - In aws76 the plate has a cut-out notch at its inner front corner, around the side-plate foot.
- **Front.** Each pod's front end carries a printed wedge/pyramid block: a triangular nose whose faces slope down and outward to the fork root.
  - Two silver bolts with nyloc nuts run horizontally through the side of that block, clamping the fork. They show on both the outer and inner faces.
- **Wheels.** Rear outer corner, outboard of the pod side wall.
  - The axle is roughly 1/4 of the pod length from the rear.
  - The rear of the pod ends around the wheel axle line, so the wheel sticks out behind and to the side.

## Forks (2)
- **Shape and position.** One long tapered spike per pod. Each comes out of the bottom of the pod nose block, runs forward and down, and its tip rests on the floor.
  - The plan view is a long thin triangle: base width about the pod width × 0.5, tapering to a blunt point.
  - Length past the pod front is about 0.6 to 0.7 of the pod length.
  - In side view the top surface ramps from about pod-mid height at the root down to the floor at the tip. The underside is flat on the floor.
- **Make.** Printed, with visible infill lines. Held by the 2 through-bolts in the nose block, so they are replaceable.
- **Alignment.** Parallel, in line with each pod's centre.

## Side plates (uprights) with horns
- **Plates.** A vertical plate rises from each pod's inner edge, next to the slot.
  - It is about 3 mm thick, black (printed or carbon), and stands about 1 pod height above the deck.
  - The upper front is rounded around the weapon axle.
- **Horns.** A curved shark-fin horn rises from each plate's top-rear and curves up and backward. The horn tip is about level with the disc top in aws72/76.
  - aws76 shows two horns on each side, one on the plate and one further back, and a printed horn with a knurled tip.
- **Axle and motor mounting.**
  - The axle passes through the plates at about 1 pod height above the deck. A big silver nyloc nut and washer sits on the outer face of one plate (aws72).
  - The other plate carries the motor: 2 to 3 socket screws around a central hole on a circular boss (aws76 shows 2 cap screws, 1 central bearing and 1 small hole).
- **Fixing.** The plates are bolted or slotted into the pod inner walls.

## Weapon
- **Direct drive.** The vertical disc spins about the x axis, mounted directly on the outrunner bell. The motor is clamped to one side plate.
- **Size and position.** The disc sits in the slot, offset to one side, with the motor between it and the opposite plate.
  - Disc diameter is about 0.6 to 0.7 of the pod length.
  - The bottom of the disc runs low in the slot, near the floor. The top stands well above the horns.
  - The front edge is roughly level with the pod noses.
- **Disc shape by event.**
  - aws72: a 2-tooth eccentric disc with a big round lightening hole (ring-like).
  - aws74: a multi-tooth (about 8) saw-tooth ring with spoke cut-outs.
  - aws76: a single large curved hook tooth with a triangular cut-out.
- **Material and thickness.** About 3 mm hardened steel, black with ground bright edges.
- **Motor.** About 18 mm diameter and 15 mm long, the same width as the disc offset from the plate. Red (aws72/74) or black (aws76).

## Electronics
- Inside the pods, hidden under the deck plates.
- Wires (black, with a red JST plug) show in the slot behind the motor and on top of the right pod (aws72).
- The power switch is not visible. It is probably on the underside or the rear.

## How things are held
- **Deck plates:** 4 screws each, into the pod walls.
- **Forks:** 2 horizontal bolts and nyloc nuts through each pod nose.
- **Side plates:** bolted to the pods; they carry the axle and motor.
- **Wheels:** on gearmotor shafts poking out of the pod outer walls at the rear. The wheels are printed, with a knurled TPU-like tyre and a 3-spoke hub.
