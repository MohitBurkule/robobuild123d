# PLA-st is a Perfectly Legitimate Choice of Material (slug `pla_st_is_a_perfectly_legitima`, listed as `pla_st` in hard20): reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/pla-st_is_a_perfectly_legitimate_choice_of_material.aws63.webp`, `...aws65.webp`, `...aws67.webp`. AWS63 and AWS65 are uncut photos on a table; AWS67 is cut out (the green edges are a cut-out halo).

Robot type: a **4-wheel mecanum-drive** robot built in **grey PLA**. It has a long **twin-rail arch arm** running over the top from the rear to the front, ending at a **bent aluminium front wedge/scoop**. The arm and wedge likely form a **lifting wedge / lifter** *(guess, see Mechanism)*. The body is an open-frame cellular box with **clear polycarbonate** cover panels.

Conventions: **front** = the aluminium wedge end. **W** = chassis width over the wheel pods (outer frame), excluding the wedge flare. Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Main structure
- **Chassis frame**: a dark-grey printed open box, about W wide, 1.1 W long and 0.35 W tall. In plan it is divided into a **2 × 3 grid of cells** by internal walls:
  - four **corner wheel pockets**, each about 0.3 W × 0.35 W, with one mecanum wheel in each;
  - a **centre spine** about 0.4 W wide, holding the electronics (red/orange silicone battery wires, a red connector, a receiver/ESC board with white/orange/brown servo leads, and a red PCB in AWS65).
  - The walls are about 0.03 W thick. The top faces of some cells carry **clear polycarbonate cover plates**, each held by **2–4 silver Phillips screws** at the corners: the rear-left cell, rear-right cell, centre front and the side cells.
- **Mecanum wheels (×4)**: at the four corners. Each is Ø about 0.32 W and 0.15 W wide, with a **grey printed hub** (a cog-like spoked disc with about 8 notches) and **about 8–10 small black/dark-grey barrel rollers**. The rollers are set at **45°** around the rim, carried between the hub's two side flanges on short pins; each roller is about 0.06 W in diameter and 0.12 W long. Some rollers have light grey/white printed end-caps. Each wheel is driven by a **small brass-gear gearmotor** (N20-style; AWS67 shows a **brass pinion/shaft** and a black **spur gear** next to the front-left wheel, so possibly a short gear reduction) *(guess)*. The wheels sit inside their pockets with about 1/3 of their height above the frame top, and they bulge out of the sides.
- **Arm (twin-rail arch)**: two **parallel grey printed rails**, each about 0.06 W wide and 0.06 W tall in section, with about 0.06 W between them (outer width about 0.18 W). They run along the **centreline** from a **raised rear block** at the rear of the chassis forward to the front. The profile is an **arch**: highest at the rear (about 0.25 W above the frame top), then sloping down toward the front. In AWS65 the rails are **wider apart at the rear** and taper together toward the front, and a cross-tie at the rear forms a "U". Each rail has **round holes** about every 0.08 W along its length (lightening/fixing holes). One rail in AWS67 has a **textured/ribbed top surface**.
  - **Front end**: the rails join a **grey block** about 0.25 W wide with a **hatched/infilled square** in the middle and **two silver Phillips screws**, one each side. The block sits just behind the top of the aluminium wedge and is screwed to it, or to a bracket that carries it.
  - **Rear end**: the rails drop down into a **rear block/hinge** at the rear centre. AWS67 shows a **grey horn / servo output** at the rear left (a short grey cylinder with a black hex nut, about 0.1 W) next to a dark rear pillar. This is most likely the **servo or pivot** driving the arm *(guess)*.
- **Front wedge / scoop**: **aluminium sheet** (scratched, possibly with tape or foil layers; AWS63 looks taped grey). It is bent into **three facets** in plan: a **flat centre panel** about 0.5 W wide, and two **side facets angled back at about 30–35°**, making a shallow chevron/"bulldozer" blade about 1.1 W wide. It slopes from about the frame top height down to the floor at about 45–55°. It is fixed with **4 silver Torx/Phillips screws** in a 2×2 square in the centre panel, plus **two open holes** lower down (unused or rivet holes). It sits **in front of the chassis**, overlapping the front wheel pockets. It is about 0.35 W tall in elevation and reaches about 0.25 W ahead of the frame.
- **Side guards**: AWS67 shows thin **green-tinted clear polycarbonate side plates** (the green is partly cut-out halo) on the outside of the chassis sides, held by 2 screws. AWS65 shows clear polycarbonate **flat side skirts** angled out over the right wheels, and a clear **front-right flap**.

## Mechanism (inferred)
- **Drive**: 4 independent mecanum wheels give holonomic motion (strafing). The PLA chassis is the robot's namesake.
- **Arm**: the arm is pivoted at the **rear** and its front end is fixed to the top of the wedge. A servo at the rear could **lift the whole wedge** (a lifting wedge). Or the rails could be a **fixed roll bar** that ties the wedge to the rear for stiffness. The rear servo-like horn and the single fixed block at the front suggest a powered arm, but this is unconfirmed *(guess)*.
