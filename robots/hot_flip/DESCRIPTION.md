# Hot Flip: reference description

Photo: `reference/hot_flip.aws71.webp` (one photo, front-left three-quarter view from above, background removed).

## Overall form
- A closed, faceted, gem-like printed shell. No wheels, screws or electronics are visible from this side: everything sits inside.
- Two greens: a light mint green (#2fe0b8-ish) on the big front/top panel and its edge rails, and a darker bottle green (#0f5f4a to #1a8a6a, shaded) on the side facets. Matte FDM print with visible layer lines.
- Plan: about square (roughly 100 x 100 mm). Height is about half to 0.6 of the length.

## Front panel (the weapon: hinged wedge / lifter panel)
- One large flat panel making up nearly the whole front face. It runs from the floor at the front edge up and back to the top of the robot, at about 35-45 degrees.
- Trapezoid: widest at the floor (close to the full robot width), narrowing towards the top (about 0.55-0.6 of the bottom width). The top edge meets a short flat top panel.
- Front lower edge sits on the ground: it is the wedge lip.
- Long edges carry thin raised lips or rails (about 2-3 mm), so the panel reads as a separate plate set into the body.
- Mechanism (user note): the panel is hinged along its top rear edge and lifts. It is a wedge-lifter or flipper: an actuator inside lifts the front lip. The hinge is hidden under the top panel.

## Top panel
- A short, flat, light-green rectangle behind the front panel's top edge, about 0.2 of the length. It has the same width as the front panel's top edge.
- Two small pins or screw heads show at its rear corners, probably the hinge pin ends.

## Sides: triangular sloping panels
- The sides are not vertical. Each side is made of dark-green facets that lean inwards from the floor towards the narrower top, so the front view is a trapezoid.
- The large facet next to the front panel is triangular-ish: wide at the floor, narrowing as it rises along the front panel edge.
- A narrow vertical strip runs at the bottom of each side. Behind it, rear facets chamfer the rear corners, so the plan is a hexagon with clipped rear corners.
- There is a small round hole or screw near the bottom rear of the left side facet, and another near its top. These are probably axle or fixing points.

## Wheels and drive
- Not visible. A two-wheel drive is hidden inside the shell, with the wheels at the rear under the high part, inside the outline.

## Proportions and relations
- Front panel: about 0.85 of the length in plan projection and about 0.9 of the width at the floor.
- Top panel: about 0.2 of the length.
- Side facets: they take up the width lost as the body narrows from bottom to top, about 20 mm each side at the top.
- No graphics or text.
