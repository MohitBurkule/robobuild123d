## 1. One-paragraph overview
Pantherk II is a **flat, square, four-wheel box rammer**. The body is a printed box about **80 × 80 × 22 mm**. A **top lid** carries a **laminated picture** of a "panther-man" gladiator. A **thicker transverse front block** spans the front, and from under its two ends **two sloped wedge prongs** project forward, leaving a **wide notch** in the middle, so the front is shaped like a "U" or fork. Four **black gear-tread wheels** sit at the four corners in notches, and the body bulges out as **side pods** between them. In AWS66 and AWS68 (and on the left of AWS67), **long thin vertical side blades** run outside the wheels and extend past the front corners. **No active weapon** is visible: it is a pusher/wedge.

## 2. Part-by-part

### 2.1 Body box (BX)
- **Shape**: a rectangular tub, about **80 mm wide × 80 mm long × 20–22 mm tall** (0.27 W). The vertical walls are about 2.5 mm thick. The four corners are **notched out** to take the wheels: each notch is about **12 mm wide (X) × 34 mm long (Y)**, so the main box between the notches is a cross-shape in plan. The **side pods SPD** are the side-wall portions between the front and rear notches.
- **Colour**: AWS66 white/ivory PLA. AWS67 **translucent glossy blue** (PETG-like, horizontal layer lines). AWS68 **translucent purple/magenta**.
- **Side wall details (AWS67, left)**: **3 small silver button/pan screws** in a row along the side pod at mid-height, about 15 mm apart (Ø about 3 mm heads). These probably hold the motor brackets or the lid posts **(guess)**.
- **Floor**: not seen. Assumed flat at about z = 3 mm (ground clearance set by the wheels) **(guess)**.

### 2.2 Side pods (SPD-L/R)
- The side wall between the wheel notches bulges out to about **x = ±46 mm** (6 mm outboard of the lid edge), with a rounded/chamfered top edge. Each is about **26 mm long × 18 mm tall**.
- In AWS67 the right pod front shows a **flat blue block** reaching into the gap between the front-right and rear-right wheels.
- **Function (guess)**: houses the two N20 gearmotors per side, with shafts pointing out into the wheels.
- Gap between the pod face and the wheel inner face: about **1–2 mm**.

### 2.3 Top lid (LD) and picture (PIC)
- **Shape**: a flat rectangular plate, about **72 × 66 mm × 2 mm**, recessed slightly into the body top (its top is about 1 mm above the wall tops).
- **Colour**: AWS66 **white**, with a photo of a gladiator (a man with a sword and helmet) covering about 85% of it. AWS67 **black**, with a photo of a **panther-headed man with a sword** (pink/brown background), about 62 × 60 mm, edge torn. AWS68 **black**, with a cut-out of a **muscular panther-man** on a lilac background, about 40 × 30 mm, centred.
- **Picture**: a paper print glued and probably laminated with tape (glossy) **(guess)**.
- **Screws (S-LD)**: small **black pan-head Phillips**, head Ø about 3 mm.
  - AWS66: one near each lid corner (4) plus one at mid-top edge (5).
  - AWS67: about 8 around the border, 2–3 per long side.
  - AWS68: about 6, at the four corners plus mid-top and mid-right.
  - They screw into posts in the body corners **(guess)**.

### 2.4 Lid border frame / clamp strips (FR) and front tabs (TB)
- AWS67: a **black frame strip** about 6 mm wide around the picture: a raised border in the same black printed lid, with the screws in it. At the **front edge** of the lid there is a **wider black transverse strip** (about 10 mm wide, full lid width) with a **raised rectangular tab TB** at **each end** (about 10 × 8 × 4 mm). The tabs look like **cable/lid retainers or self-righting nubs** **(guess)**.
- AWS68: the same front strip with **two raised black tabs**. The right tab has a small square marked with an "X" (a screw recess or a power LED cover) **(guess)**.

### 2.5 Power switch (SW)
- AWS67: a **small rectangular opening** at the **right-rear of the lid**, at the lid border, showing a **red component** (switch lever or LED).
- AWS68: a **slide switch** visible in a **rectangular slot about 8 × 4 mm** at the **right side of the lid**, near the middle.

### 2.6 Front block (FB)
- **Shape**: a **transverse rectangular bar** across the full body front, about **80 mm wide × 16 mm deep (Y) × 22 mm tall**. Its front face is vertical and its top is flush with or slightly above the lid.
- AWS66: a white block with **"PANTHERK II"** hand-written in black marker on its sloped front-top face, letters about 8 mm tall. It is **wider than the lid** and protrudes about **6 mm beyond each side**, flush with the wheel outer faces.
- AWS67/68: a blue/purple block with **3 silver screws** in a row across the front face at mid-height (x ≈ −20, 0, +20 mm), Ø about 4 mm heads. In AWS68 these are **hex socket button heads**.
- **Function**: the front bumper and the mounting for the prongs (the screws probably fix the prong bodies or the front bumper to the box) **(guess)**.

### 2.7 Front prongs (PR-L/R)
- **Shape**: a **sloped wedge** (doorstop). The back is the same height as FB (about 20 mm). The bottom is flat on the floor. The top slopes at about **25–30°** to a floor-level front edge. Each is about **22 mm wide (X) × 34 mm long (Y)**.
  - AWS67: the top face shows a **raised rectangular step/rib**, so the face is **stepped** (two-level). The outer side faces are vertical. The inner sides are vertical too and face each other across the notch. The top step has a lip at the back.
- **Position**: PR-L at x ≈ −29 mm (−40 to −18), PR-R at x ≈ +29 mm. The **notch between them is about 36 mm wide** (0.45 W) and about 34 mm deep (the full prong length). The prong back faces **join FB's front face** (printed with FB, or screwed through FB) **(guess)**.
- **Tape (TP)**: AWS68 shows **clear/white packing tape strips** running down each prong face and **overhanging the tip by about 8–10 mm**, lying flat on the floor. That gives a thin, sharp leading edge (a "tape skirt"). AWS67 prongs look taped too (shiny).
- **Relative**: the prong length (34 mm) is about the body height × 1.6. The two prongs together are about 55% of the body width.

### 2.8 Wheels (WH)
- **Shape**: **Ø about 30 mm × 9 mm wide**, with a **coarse knurled/gear-tooth tread** (about 36–40 square teeth, each about 1.5 mm). The outer face has **3 round or kidney holes** (Ø about 7 mm) around a hub (about Ø 8 mm).
- **Colour**: black (AWS66/67), dark grey (AWS68).
- **Positions** (centres): x ≈ ±(40 − 6 + 4.5) ≈ **±38 mm** (inside the corner notches, so the outer faces are flush with FB/the body outer line, at x ≈ ±43). **y ≈ +22 mm (front pair) and y ≈ −24 mm (rear pair)**, z ≈ 15 mm. The **wheelbase is about 46 mm** and the **front/rear tyre gap is about 16 mm** (occupied by the side pod).
- **Height**: Ø 30 against a body height of 22, so the wheels stand **about 4 mm above the lid and about 4 mm below the floor** **(guess)**, giving invertible running.
- **Clearances**: about 1–2 mm to the notch walls front, back and inside.
- **Drive (guess)**: 4 × N20 gearmotors, direct drive, skid steer.

### 2.9 Side blades (SB-L/R)
- **AWS66**: **long thin grey translucent strips**, about **2–3 mm thick, 15–20 mm tall, about 110 mm long**, mounted **vertically** just outside the wheels on both sides (x ≈ ±48 mm). They run from about the rear wheel centre to about **30 mm ahead of the front block**, ending in an **angled point at the floor**. The left blade is **splayed outward** at the front by about 10°. These look like **polycarbonate or aluminium sheet**.
- **AWS67**: only the **left** side has a **blue translucent side skirt/wedge**: about 2 mm thick and as tall as the body at the rear, sweeping **forward and down** to a pointed tip about 12 mm ahead of the front-left wheel. It is mounted outside the left wheels. The right side has none.
- **AWS68**: **black flat vertical plates** on **both** sides, outboard of the wheels: about **100 mm long × 20 mm tall × 2–3 mm thick**, from just behind the rear wheels to just ahead of the front block. They look like **wheel guards** and anti-flip side skirts.
- **Mount (guess)**: screwed to the ends of the side pods, or to the axle stub ends, with standoffs through the gap between the wheels (no fasteners are visible at this resolution). The gap between the blade inner face and the wheel outer face is about **2–3 mm**.

## 6. Mechanism
- **Drive**: four wheels, skid steer, invertible (the wheels stick out top and bottom).
- **Weapon**: passive: **two wedge prongs** to get under opponents, then push. The notch between the prongs may trap a spinner's or opponent's wedge. The side blades stop the robot being flanked or flipped from the side.
- No servo or spinner is visible.

---

## 7. Assembly order
1. Print the body BX with the side pods, the corner notches and the front bar FB (with prongs) **(guess: one piece)**.
2. Install 4 gearmotors in the side pods, shafts outward, and press the wheels onto the shafts.
3. Install the battery, receiver, ESC and switch. Feed the switch through the lid slot.
4. Screw on the lid with 5–8 black screws. Glue the picture on top. Add the black front strip/tabs.
5. Screw the 3 front screws through FB.
6. Fit the side blades outside the wheels (screws/standoffs into the pods).
7. Tape the prong faces with packing tape, overhanging the tips.

## 13. Consistency check
1. **Wheel Ø**: the AWS67 pixel estimate (16 mm) conflicts with the ratio estimate (30 mm). The wheel ellipses in AWS67 are seen obliquely and partly hidden by the body. The AWS68 front view shows the wheels' visible height about 0.35 of the body length. Keep **Ø 30**; it must exceed the body height (22) for the visible above-and-below protrusion.
2. **Prong width vs notch**: 2 × 20 + 38 = 78 ≈ 80. Consistent; use prongs 21 + notch 38 + 21.
3. **Prong length**: AWS67 shows the prongs about as long as the front wheel spacing, about 35–40. **Use 38.**
4. **Front block wider than the body (AWS66)**: the AWS66 block spans x 60–430 vs lid 115–385, i.e. 1.37 × lid width, so the block is about 90 mm wide, sticking out about 5 mm each side over the wheel fronts. In AWS67/68 the block equals the body width. **Version difference**; the default model uses 80.
5. **Side blades**: present in 66 (both sides), 67 (left only) and 68 (both, black). **Default to AWS68's black blades on both sides.**

## 14. Why the designer did it
- **Low square box**: hard to get under and stable. A square footprint fits the 4 in cube, and the low height presents little to vertical spinners. The weak point is the square sides vs horizontal spinners. Easy one-piece print.
- **Picture lid**: identity and showmanship. The lid is sacrificial and removable for maintenance (screws). The black lid hides the wiring.
- **Raised tabs / front strip on the lid**: possibly cable retainers or stops that keep the lid from flexing. They may act as **self-righting bumps**; with the wheels sticking out both sides that is unneeded **(guess)**.
- **Front block with 3 screws**: the strongest part of the body takes ramming. Screws hold the prong unit so damaged prongs can be replaced quickly **(guess)**.
- **Two prongs + notch**: the prongs get under opponents at two points and are less likely to be blocked by an opponent's single wedge tip. The **notch** may trap an opponent's wedge or fork tip, so a wedge-vs-wedge meeting becomes "my prongs under your sides". Tape overhangs make a thin, sharp skirt that gets lower than printed plastic (which cannot be printed sharp). The weak point is that the printed prong tips can be chewed by spinners; the tape is cheap to replace.
- **Corner-notched wheels, Ø 30 > body height**: invertible driving and protection on 3 sides by the body. Gear tread for grip. The weak point is the exposed outer faces, which is why the **side blades** were added.
- **Side blades**: shield the wheel faces from horizontal spinners and side-on wedges. The pointed noses act as extra fork tips at the outer corners (wider front coverage). They are thin to save weight. The weak point: a thin blade can bend into a wheel and jam it. The 2–3 mm gap must be kept.
- **Switch on the lid side**: reachable for arming at the arena, and recessed so it is not knocked off.
- **Colour change per event**: a reprint after damage (cheap and repeatable), using the filament on hand.
