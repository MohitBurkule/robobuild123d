# Pantherk II (slug `pantherk`): reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/pantherk_ii.aws66.webp`, `pantherk_ii.aws67.webp`, `pantherk_ii.aws68.webp` (AWS wiki).

Robot type: **4-wheel box pusher/rammer with twin front wedge prongs**. No active weapon is visible. The same basic design was reprinted in a different colour for each event: **white** (AWS66), **translucent blue** (AWS67) and **translucent purple** (AWS68).

Conventions: **front** = the end with the two prongs (toward the camera in AWS66/68, toward the lower-left in AWS67). **W** = width of the main body box, measured without wheels. Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Common layout (all three photos)
- **Main body**: a flat, nearly square box. Plan size is about W × 1.0 W and height about 0.25–0.3 W. It is printed plastic. The rectangular **top lid** is inset slightly, with a **printed paper picture** glued or laminated on top: a gladiator (AWS66), a panther-headed man with a sword (AWS67), or a muscular panther-man cut-out on lilac (AWS68). The lid has **4–8 small black screws** around its edge (one near each corner, plus some mid-edge).
- **Four wheels**, two per side, outboard of the body, at the **front and rear corners** (wheelbase about 0.85 W). They are black, with a **coarse knurled/gear-tooth tread** (about 40 teeth). Diameter is about 0.33–0.38 W, slightly more than the body height, so they stick out above and below the body (invertible). Width is about 0.1 W. Each face has **3 round/kidney holes** (a 3-spoke hub). They sit in **cut-outs/notches at the body corners**: the body side between the wheels bulges out as a **side pod**, about 0.08 W proud, which carries the motor *(guess: N20 gearmotor per wheel, direct drive)*.
- **Front block**: a thicker transverse bar, full body width × about 0.2 W deep, slightly taller than the lid. It spans the front of the body and carries the robot name ("PANTHERK II" hand-written in marker in AWS66). In AWS67/68 the front face has **3 silver screws** in a row (AWS68 shows them as hex socket screws, evenly spaced, at about 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 W across).
- **Two front wedge prongs**: one under each front corner, sticking forward from the front block. Each is a **sloped wedge** about 0.25 W wide that reaches about 0.4–0.45 W ahead of the front block, with its tip on the floor. The face slope is about 25–30°. The gap between prongs is about 0.4 W (a wide notch in the middle, so the robot's front looks like a "U" or fork from above). The faces are covered in **clear tape** (AWS68 clearly shows white or clear packing-tape strips extending past the plastic). In AWS67 they are printed blue with a stepped/ridged top face.
- **Two black rubber-ish strips** on the top lid (AWS67/68): transverse flat bars, about 0.1 W wide, near the front edge of the lid. Each has a **small raised ramp/tab** at the outer end (AWS67 shows two black block tabs at the lid front corners). These are probably lid clamps or anti-wedge lips *(guess)*.

## Image 1: `pantherk_ii.aws66.webp` (AWS 66, white)
- View: from the front, above (about 60° down), on a cardboard box. The image is low resolution and blurry.
- The body is **white/cream printed**. The gladiator photo lid is about 0.85 W square, with a black screw at each corner and one mid-top.
- The front block is white, full width, about 0.25 W deep, with the robot name drawn on its front/top face.
- **Long thin grey side plates**: on each side, outboard of the wheels, a **thin grey translucent strip/blade** (polycarbonate or aluminium, about 0.03 W wide in plan, edge-up) runs the full length and **extends past the front** by about 0.35 W to a tip near the floor. The left one is angled outward. These are side wedgelets or wheel guards, mounted vertically to the outside of the wheels *(guess: bolted to the axle ends or side pods)*.
- No separate front prongs are visible in this version (the side blades may act as forks).

## Image 2: `pantherk_ii.aws67.webp` (AWS 67, translucent blue)
- View: front-left three-quarter, elevated about 40°.
- The body is **translucent glossy blue** with horizontal layer lines. The left side wall has **3 small silver button screws** along it at mid-height.
- The lid is **black** with a **picture sheet** about 0.75 W square, and a black border frame strip around it with 2–4 screws per side.
- **The two blue prongs are clearly visible**: each is a hollow-looking translucent wedge with a stepped/ribbed top, about 0.28 W wide and 0.4 W long. They attach under the front of the body at the left and right ends. The inside edges are vertical, so the prongs read as two separate "teeth".
- **Left side**: a **thin blue translucent side skirt/wedge** runs along the left side outboard of the wheels and sweeps forward and down to a point ahead of the front-left wheel. It is about 0.03 W thick and as tall as the body.
- **Right side**: the side pod between the wheels is a blue block. The wheel hubs show **3 oval holes**.
- At the right rear of the lid there is a small opening with a **red component** (a switch or LED; probably the power switch/link).

## Image 3: `pantherk_ii.aws68.webp` (AWS 68, translucent purple)
- View: straight from the front, elevated about 50°, on a table.
- The body is **translucent purple/magenta**. The lid is **black** with the panther-man cut-out on a lilac background. There is a **small rectangular switch opening** at the right of the lid (a slide switch).
- **Black clamp strips** sit across the front of the lid, with **raised black tabs at both ends**. The right tab has a small **"X"-marked square** (a screw hole or cross recess).
- The front block is purple, with **3 silver socket screws** in a row on its front face.
- **Prongs**: two purple wedges, each with a long **clear/white tape strip** hanging over the tip (the tape extends about 0.1 W beyond the plastic and sits flat on the floor).
- **Side blades**: on both sides, **long black flat vertical plates**, outboard of both wheels and as long as the body plus about 0.15 W, about 0.3 W tall. They guard the wheels, or are side wedges. They are attached at the wheel axle line *(guess)*.
- The wheels are dark grey tread with gear-tooth knurls. A small wooden stick can be seen at the top-left (a background object).

## Relative sizes (to body width W)
| Part | Size |
|---|---|
| Body box | W × ~1.0 W × 0.28 W tall |
| Lid picture panel | ~0.8 W square |
| Front block | W × 0.2 W × 0.3 W |
| Front prongs (×2) | 0.25–0.28 W wide × 0.4 W forward reach |
| Gap between prongs | ~0.4 W |
| Wheels (×4) | Ø 0.35 W × 0.1 W |
| Side blades (AWS66/68) | ~1.2–1.4 W long × 0.3 W tall × 0.02 W thick |

The overall length (prong tip to rear) is about 1.45 W. The overall width over the side blades is about 1.35 W.

## Hidden / uncertain
- The motors (4 × N20 *guess*), battery and receiver are hidden in the box.
- The underside is not visible.
- How the side blades attach is unknown: possibly screwed to the side pods.
- Whether the black lid strips are separate parts or part of the lid.
- The AWS66 version may lack front prongs; the resolution is too poor to be sure.
