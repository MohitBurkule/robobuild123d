# RBMK: reference photo description

Photos: `robots/rbmk/reference/rbmk.aws71.webp` (front-right iso, ~35 deg up), `rbmk.aws74.webp` (straight front, ~25 deg up), `rbmk.aws76.webp` (front-left iso, ~45 deg up). Backgrounds are cut out (green fringe on the edges is a masking artefact, not paint).

Scale: the motors are taken as 1806-class outrunners (~21-23 mm across). That gives about 0.10-0.11 mm per pixel in aws74, so the body box is about 55-58 mm wide.

## Overall layout
- A low, flat, rectangular aluminium box sits in the middle.
- One folded aluminium base sheet runs under it and grows three features:
  - a left wing and a right wing that fold down and outward at the front half of the box sides;
  - a tall up-turned rear plate behind the box.
- Each wing carries a black outrunner motor with a long black bar weapon bolted on top of its bell, so there are two horizontal spinners, one each side.
- At the front centre, two blue rollers sit side by side on one axle in a small aluminium fork.
- There are no drive wheels and no legs. The robot moves only by changing the speeds of the two spinners (gyro and vibration "shuffler").
- It is left-right symmetric apart from the bars' rotation angles in each photo.

## Body box (centre)
- Shape: a rectangular tub, a little longer than wide (about 58 W x 64 L x 13 H mm), made of brushed / scratched bare aluminium.
- Top edges along the left and right sides are chamfered at about 45 deg. aws76 shows a sloped strip between the lid and the side wall, about 4 mm wide.
- The front face is flat and vertical. The rear face is hidden by the rear plate.
- Lid: a clear, slightly milky / scratched polycarbonate sheet covering the top. Electronics show through it faintly: a red battery lead and white connector in aws71 and aws76, and a dark board.
- Fixings: 4 silver button-head socket screws, one near each lid corner. They are inset about 6-8 mm from the corners and hold the lid down into the box.
- Colour: aluminium silver-grey; the lid is clear with a grey tint.

## Base sheet, wings and rear plate (one folded aluminium sheet, ~1 mm)
- Wings:
  - They leave the bottom edge of the box sides from about the box's mid-length to its front corner.
  - They slope down outward at about 10 deg. In aws74 the outer tips sit lower than the box bottom, and the wing tips are the skids that touch the floor.
  - Plan shape: a trapezoid that is widest at the box side. The front and rear edges angle inward toward the tip, and the outer edge is cut straight.
  - Reach: about 25-28 mm outboard of the box side.
- Rear plate:
  - It rises from the back bottom edge of the box, tilted back at roughly 40-50 deg from horizontal.
  - Its top edge stands about 10-15 mm above the lid.
  - Outline: a pentagon / hexagon. The sides are full box width near the fold, then taper to a narrower flat top, about half the width.
  - It acts as a rear wedge / self-righting ramp and frames the box in every photo.
- Colour: the same bare, scuffed aluminium.

## Weapon motors (2)
- Two black-anodised outrunners with a silver ring band. Copper windings show through the bell's spoke openings. The left one has a white "Le Todaro" style label.
- Each motor stands on its wing just outboard of the box side, slightly forward of the box centre. The motor axis is perpendicular to the wing, so it tilts outward about 10 deg.
- The motor diameter is about 40 % of the box width. The motor height is about the box height.
- Held by: screws through the wing into the stator base (not visible, implied).

## Weapon bars (2)
- Flat dark-grey / black steel (or hardened steel) bars, about 1.5-2 mm thick and about 9 mm wide.
- Length tip to tip is about 1.2-1.3x the box width (~70 mm).
- Each end has a T-shaped cross piece, about 20-24 mm wide and about 6 mm deep, with one corner hooked or bevelled as the impact tooth.
- Each arm has a long through slot (lightening slot) along its centre line, from near the hub to near the T.
- Hub: a ring of 4 small silver Torx / socket screws on a ~9 mm circle around a centre boss / screw. It sits directly on the motor bell, so the bar is bolted to the bell with no top support.
- Height: the bar's plane passes just above the lid. The inner half of each bar sweeps over the box top, and the two sweeps do not overlap in the middle.
- Each bar spins in its own tilted plane, following its motor's tilt.

## Nose rollers (front centre)
- Two bright blue polyurethane rollers, each about 12-14 mm in diameter and about 11 mm wide, on one horizontal axle across the robot.
- They are mounted in a small fork of three vertical aluminium plates (left, centre, right). The plates are bolted to the box front with small socket screws, two visible on the front face, and the axle ends show nuts / screws on the outer plates.
- The rollers stand just in front of the box front face. Their bottoms are about level with the wing tips (floor contact).
- They are passive: no motor is visible. They let the nose slide or roll while the robot shuffles.
- In aws76 the outer fork plate has a rounded / D-shaped outline.

## Graphics
- None: bare aluminium, a clear lid, black motors and bars, and blue rollers. There are no decals or text on the body.

## Mechanism (inferred)
- Electronics in the box: a 2S LiPo, a receiver, and two brushless weapon ESCs. The switch is not visible in the photos.
- Motion: by differential speed of the two spinners only. Speeding one up or slowing it down makes a reaction torque and gyro precession. That rocks the robot on its wing tips and rollers, and it walks / skates.

## Relative sizes (box width = 1.0)
- Box length about 1.1, box height about 0.23.
- Wing reach about 0.45 each side.
- Rear plate rise above the lid about 0.25.
- Motor diameter about 0.38.
- Bar length about 1.2-1.3, bar width about 0.16.
- Roller diameter about 0.23.
