# Delta V: reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/delta_v.aws72.webp`, `delta_v.aws74.webp`, `delta_v.aws76.webp` (AWS wiki, cut out; the green tint on the AWS74 body is a colour cast, not paint, since the body is black).

Robot type: **compact vertical spinner**. The weapon is a **single-tooth (asymmetric) yellow disc/beater** on a brushless outrunner, mounted on the centreline in a slot between **two front wedge pods**. The robot has two-wheel drive, with wheels at the rear corners. It is all **matte black FDM** except the yellow weapon and the eye stickers. The two front pods carry "sleepy eye" stickers, giving it a face.

Conventions: **front** = the pods/forks end. **W** = overall body width (side plate to side plate). Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Main parts (all photos)
1. **Body**: a black box about W wide × 0.9 W long × 0.3 W tall at the rear. The top is flat, with a rectangular **inset lid panel** on each side half, held by small black screws. A small **white switch/connector** with a rectangular slot sits at the rear right (AWS76).
2. **Side plates (×2)**: black **stadium-shaped** vertical plates, about 0.04 W thick, 0.8 W long and 0.3 W tall, on the left and right outer faces. Each has **3–4 small black screws** in a row (top-front, mid, bottom-rear). They shroud the drive wheels.
3. **Drive wheels (×2)**: one per side at the **rear corners**, inside the side plates. Each is Ø about 0.3 W and 0.1 W wide. They are **yellow/cream foam-like or printed TPU tyres** with a speckled texture, on hubs with **3 round holes**. The wheels stick up above the body top by about 0.05 W at the rear and are visible through cut-outs in the top/rear corners. The drive motors are presumably N20 gearmotors inside the rear *(guess)*. No front wheels are visible; the front rests on the forks or wedge.
4. **Front pods (×2)**: left and right, each about 0.35 W wide. Each has a **sloped top face** (about 35°) running down to the front. A central **slot about 0.25 W wide** between the pods houses the weapon's lower half. On each pod:
   - a **round "eye" sticker** (white disc with a black half-moon pupil and a heavy lid line, about 0.12 W in diameter) on the upper sloped facet;
   - **2–3 shallow oval recesses / screw pockets** on the lower facet, with black screws in two of them (fixing the forks or wedge plate).
5. **Weapon tower / bearing support**: a **black printed upright** about 0.2 W thick and 0.35 W tall above the body, with a rounded "D" top. It stands on the **right** of the weapon (robot's right side, seen in AWS76). On its outer face is a **4-screw pattern plus a centre hole** (the motor mount: the stator bolted to the tower). On the **left** of the disc, AWS72/74 show a **separate black cylindrical bearing housing** about 0.18 W in diameter, carrying a **steel ball bearing** with a **threaded shaft end/nut** protruding. That is the outboard support of the weapon axle on a smaller pillar.
6. **Weapon**: a **flat yellow (painted or anodised) plate**, about 0.04 W thick, with an overall diameter of about 0.6 W. The outline is **a round boss with one big hooked tooth**: from a circular hub, one side sweeps out into a large curved hook/spike. The tooth has a **triangular lightening hole** near its tip. The plate is bolted with **4 black socket screws/nuts** to a black **outrunner bell / hub** (black, Ø about 0.3 W, 0.15 W thick) on the transverse axle. The disc spins in the **vertical plane** along the robot's axis. About 2/3 of its height is above the body top, and it is exposed in the front slot between the pods. The black outrunner sits between the disc and the right tower, so it is a **hub/outrunner-driven** disc *(likely: the outrunner bell drives the disc directly)*.

## Front configurations (they differ by event)
- **AWS72: 4 short forks**. Each pod has **two parallel steel fork tines** (flat bar about 0.035 W wide) sticking out of slots at the pod's lower front, about 0.3 W forward. Each ends in a **barbed arrow tip** (a step plus a chisel point). Tine spacing within a pod is about 0.25 W, and the inner tines flank the weapon slot.
- **AWS74: 2 long forks**. One long tine per pod, at its **outer** edge, about 0.55 W forward, with an arrow tip. The pods are narrower and more pointed (a chisel-nose shape tapering to the fork root).
- **AWS76: full-width wedge plate**. A **single flat clear/steel blade** (scratched; looks like polycarbonate or thin steel with a green-tinted edge) spans the **full width under both pods** and the slot. It is about 1.0 W wide and reaches about 0.15 W ahead of the pods. Its front edge is tapered at the sides, so the outline is a trapezoid with chamfered front corners. It is held by the screws in the pods' oval pockets.
