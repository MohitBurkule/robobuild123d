# Dragoon: reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/dragoon.aws72.webp`, `dragoon.aws74.webp`, `dragoon.aws76.webp` (AWS wiki, cut out; a green halo shows on clear plastic edges).

Robot type: **vertical spinner**, with an outrunner-driven disc/ring in a **centre slot** that splits a **bright blue printed body** into left and right halves. The front has **black printed wedge pods** with metal forks/prongs. Drive is **2 wheels at the rear corners**, covered by **clear polycarbonate side plates**.

Conventions: **front** = the forks end. **W** = body width (outer faces of the blue halves, without the side plates). Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Common parts
1. **Body halves (×2)**: bright **blue PLA blocks**, each about 0.4 W wide × 0.9 W long × 0.3 W tall, either side of a **centre slot about 0.2 W wide**. Each top is flat at the rear and **chamfered/sloped down to the front** (a 45° bevel over the front 0.2 W). A **horizontal parting line** (lid split) runs along each block about 0.08 W below the top. Small black screws at the corners hold the top lids. A **rear cross-member** joins the halves behind the weapon (the slot does not run through at the rear). A small **rectangular cut-out** at the rear centre holds the weapon motor wires.
2. **Weapon support**: a **black printed cylinder housing** (Ø about 0.3 W, 0.3 W long, axis transverse) on one side of the slot holds the **outrunner motor**. AWS72/74 show it **right of the disc**, with a 3–4 screw end cap and **black motor wires** looping out the top. On the other side is a **black upright cheek / bearing arm** (about 0.1 W thick, 0.4 W tall, with a rounded top). In AWS76 it has a **silver Phillips axle screw**. Together they form a **yoke** carrying the weapon axle about 0.35–0.45 W above the floor.
3. **Weapon**: different per event:
   - **AWS72 / AWS74**: a **steel ring/hoop disc**, rusty/copper-brown with a scuffed face. It is an **eccentric ring** about 0.5 W in outer diameter and 0.06 W in ring width, with a **single hook tooth** on its rim. The ring is mounted off-centre on the hub, which gives a large hollow centre (an "eccentric ring beater"). It spins in a vertical plane in the slot and is bolted to the outrunner bell.
   - **AWS76**: a **flat dark steel/titanium plate disc**, Ø about 0.55 W, with **two large kidney-shaped cut-outs** that leave an **S-shaped central spoke**, plus a **single big triangular tooth** sticking out (about 0.3 W beyond the rim). The tooth has a **triangular lightening window**, and its leading edge is curved and sharp. The **outrunner (with copper windings visible)** sits inside the disc's hub area. The black cylinder housing is now **behind/right** and the black cheek arm is **in front/left**.
4. **Front pods (×2)**: **black printed wedge blocks**, one in front of each blue half. The weapon slot gap continues between them (about 0.2 W). Each is about 0.4 W wide × 0.25 W long, with a sloped top. They are fixed with **2–3 black screws** into the blue halves.
5. **Forks / prongs**: different per event:
   - **AWS72**: **4 black printed tines** (2 per pod), each about 0.08 W wide, 0.35 W long and 0.04 W thick, with **silver steel chisel tips** (about 0.1 W long, bevelled) glued or screwed at the ends. They are evenly spaced; the inner tines flank the slot.
   - **AWS74**: each pod has a **flat black ledge/skirt** (a stepped wedge plate about 0.4 W wide, chamfered corners) plus **one steel blade-fork per pod** in its centre: a **zig-zag/stepped steel strip** (about 0.04 W wide), standing on edge and pointing forward-down to a **chisel point**, held in a slot by 2 screws.
   - **AWS76**: each pod has **two thin curved steel spring prongs** (about 0.02 W wide, 0.45 W long). They curve forward and down, sweeping slightly outward, emerging from slots in the pod's front face.
6. **Side plates (×2)**: **clear polycarbonate**, about 0.02 W thick, 0.5 W long and 0.3 W tall, on the outer faces at the **rear half** of each side, with **2–4 black screws**. They cover the wheels' outer faces, and some edges are wrapped in **clear tape**.
7. **Drive wheels (×2)**: at the **rear corners**, outboard of the blue halves and inside the clear side plates. Each is Ø about 0.33 W and 0.12 W wide.
   - **AWS72**: **black knurled gear-tooth tyres** with black hubs with large round holes.
   - **AWS74/76**: **white/translucent foam or TPU tyres** on **blue hubs** with 3 round holes.
   - The wheels stick up above the body top by about 0.05 W *(so the robot is partly invertible; uncertain)*.

## Relative sizes (to W)

| Body halves (×2) | 0.4 W × 0.9 W × 0.3 W |
| Weapon slot | 0.2 W wide |
| Motor cylinder | Ø 0.3 W × 0.3 W |
| Ring disc (AWS72/74) | Ø 0.5 W, ring 0.06 W wide |
| Plate disc (AWS76) | Ø 0.55 W plus 0.3 W tooth |

| Clear side plates | 0.5 W × 0.3 W |

## Hidden / uncertain
- Drive motors, battery, ESC and receiver are inside the blue halves.
- The exact axle and bearing arrangement.
- Whether the ring disc is welded to a hub or is a hub-less ring on the outrunner's offset flange.
- Whether front skids or wheels exist: none are visible, so the front rests on the pods and forks.
