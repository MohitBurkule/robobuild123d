# Elevron Red — reference photo description

Photos: `reference/elevron_red.aws72.webp` (AWS72) and `reference/elevron_red.aws74.webp` (AWS74). Both are front-right three-quarter views from above. The user picked these cameras: AWS72 at azimuth 60 / elevation 35, AWS74 at azimuth 45 / elevation 35.

Directions are the robot's own: +y is forward (toward the blades), +x is the robot's left. "Viewer-left" means the left side of the image.

Overall: a 4-wheel antweight lifter of the Elevron 3.0 family.
- **Colours:** red PLA/PETG on top, a black printed tub, black rubber tyres.
- **Layout:** a raked black-and-red lifter arm runs along one side of the top, and a red servo cover sits on the other side. Red side panels (pods) wrap all four wheels.
- **Front:** long wedge forks in AWS74; short pods in AWS72.

## Front end: the two photos differ

- **AWS74 (later event):** each side panel runs forward from the front wheel into a long tapered fork. The forks are about as long as the chassis and end in thin points at the floor. Each fork has a small saw-tooth notch on its top edge about 2/3 of the way along. The arm blade tip finishes well behind the fork tips.
- **AWS72 (earlier event):** the side panels stop just ahead of the front wheels as short closed pods. The front face is a steep wedge, and a raised triangle is moulded on the outer face. The arm blade (with a dark steel or brown tip edge) is then the furthest-forward part, ending at the floor.
- **Choice: AWS74's long forks.** The Elevron 3.0 Alt side panel in the SolidWorks oracle has its fork tip at y 98.6, which matches AWS74. AWS72 therefore scores lower on the front third; this is a different configuration, not a wrong outline.

## Chassis tub (black)

- Low black rectangular tub between the four wheels. In the photos it is mostly hidden under the red cover, the arm and the side panels.
- Seen in the dark band between the red cover and the side pods, and in the gap between the cover and the arm.
- Top is flat and black, about level with the tyre tops (roughly 0.8–0.9 × wheel diameter tall).
- A black slide switch (body and slider) shows through an opening in the black top, viewer-left of centre, in front of the red cover's front edge (AWS72 and AWS74).
- Front: a black sloped face under the arm (AWS74, below the arm), the wedge between the forks.

## Red servo cover / housing (robot's left side in model terms, viewer-left-centre in the photos)

- **Shape:** a red printed block sitting on the black top, roughly half the chassis width and about 2/3 of the chassis length, from the rear to about the front axle.
  - Its sides are 45° chamfers: a trapezoidal section that is wider at the base.
  - The front face is a long slope down to the black top.
  - The top is a narrower flat strip.
  - A shallow recessed channel or groove runs lengthwise along the top centre, so the top reads as two raised rails (AWS72 shows a trough with sloped walls).
- **Marking:** "0 1" is engraved or debossed on the outer sloped top face (AWS74, beside the left-rear screw). The characters are small, about 5 mm tall and read along y.
- **Fasteners:** three black M2/M2.5 button-head screws on the top flange: rear-outer corner, front-outer corner and front-inner corner. The screws sit in small counterbores, with a small hole beside two of them (alignment or vent).
- **Held by:** screws into the black tub or lid below. It is a removable cover over the servo bay.
- **Size:** about 25 mm wide at the base, about 10–14 mm above the black top, and 45–50 mm long.

## Servo (black, mostly hidden)

- A micro servo (SG90/CS07 class) under or inside the red cover, with its output shaft horizontal, pointing toward the arm (sideways, along x).
- A **white plastic single-arm servo horn** stands up vertically in the gap between the cover and the arm. It is about 3 mm wide and about 12 mm tall above the spline, with a small steel screw or pin near its top. A short silver link or rod (AWS74, below the horn) runs from it to the arm.
- A black servo body edge is visible behind the horn.

## Lifter arm (black side plate + red top rail), along the other half of the top

- **Pivot:** the pivot boss is at the rear top, the highest point of the robot, above the rear of the servo cover.
  - The rear end is a black rounded boss about 16 mm in diameter.
  - A **red anodised button-head screw** sits in the boss centre (the pivot screw, with a red cap or washer).
- **Black side plates:** one thick black plate, or two black layers with a red layer sandwiched on top. Profile:
  - From the boss the top runs forward almost level (slightly down) to a **step/notch about 40% along**. There a black hook or tooth stands up and points back (AWS74 and AWS72 both show a small black notch on top at the step).
  - After the step the top drops a few mm and continues as a long straight rake down to the floor at the front.
  - The underside of the plate is a straight line from the boss to the tip. The plate is deepest (about 12 mm) behind the step and tapers toward the tip.
- **Red top rail:**
  - A red strip about 2–3 mm thick follows the whole top edge, from over the boss to the tip.
  - Its width matches the plate (about 10–12 mm) and its edges are slightly chamfered.
  - At the front the red rail ends as a separate **red wedge blade tip** about 15 mm long. The blade lies flat on the floor, with a dark steel or brown edge strip in AWS72.
- **Second red screw:** another red button-head screw on the black plate's outer face, about mid-length low down (AWS72 near the cover front, AWS74 mid-arm). It is probably the horn-link pin or a plate-clamping screw.
- **Further screw:** a dark screw near the front of the black plate (AWS74, about 3/4 along).
- **Position:** the arm sits on the robot's right half (viewer-right of the cover). Its outer face lies roughly over the inner faces of the right wheels, and its tip is on the centreline-right, between the forks.
- **Mechanism:** the servo horn pushes the arm up through the link, and the arm rotates about the rear pivot (x axis). At rest the arm lies on the chassis top, with the tip on the floor ahead of the chassis.

## Pivot stud and nut

- At the rear-outer corner of the red cover, level with its top and below and outboard of the red pivot screw, there is a **silver hex nut on a threaded steel stud**.
- The stud runs horizontally along x and sticks out about 4–5 mm past the nut, toward the outer side.
- It is the far end of the pivot bolt or of a cross bolt through the black lug that carries the arm. A black block (lug) is visible between the stud and the arm boss.

## Wheels (black)

- Four black rubber or foam tyres about 21 mm in diameter and about 9 mm wide on the hubs, with no visible hub detail.
- The tyre tops sit about level with the chassis top. The wheels are set into the side panel openings: rear pair at the rear corners, front pair at about 1/3 from the front of the tub.
- Screws or hubs show in the wheel centres (AWS72 rear-left wheel).

## Side panels / pods (red, both sides, mirrored)

- **Rear pod:**
  - A **rounded** red pod behind each rear wheel and around its rear-outer corner.
  - Outer top edges are well rounded (fillet about 3–4 mm).
  - Top is lower than the front pod (about 2/3 of the tyre height).
  - **Three vertical vent grooves** ("III") on the outer face.
- **Web:** a horizontal plate between the rear and front wheels, at about axle-top height.
  - Its top surface carries **two black screws** (AWS74 and AWS72 show screws in counterbores on the rear pod / web top inner area) going down into the tub.
  - There is also a larger round hole (about 4 mm) on the web, near the rear wheel.
- **Front pod:**
  - Wraps the front of each front wheel, with a taller box section.
  - **Five vent grooves** ("IIIII") on the outer face.
  - The top edge rounds over.
  - Forward of it the fork starts (AWS74) or a wedge face finishes (AWS72).
- The inner side of each front pod shows a small rectangular notch or tab up against the arm/chassis.
- **Colour:** red, a bit more orange in AWS74. Printed with visible layer lines and a matte finish.
