# Elevron Red: full description of the reference photos

## Conventions and sources

**Sources.** The two reference photos are in `reference/`:
- `elevron_red.aws72.webp`, called **AWS72** below (1279 × 879 px).
- `elevron_red.aws74.webp`, called **AWS74** below (1749 × 969 px).

Both photos are three-quarter views from above. The cameras are the user's picks in `REFERENCE.json`:
- AWS72: azimuth 60°, elevation 35°.
- AWS74: azimuth 45°, elevation 35°.

Both photos have the background removed and show no ruler. Every size below is a ratio to a known part, turned into millimetres through two anchors:
- **The tyres:** RV22, about 21.5 mm OD and about 9.5 mm wide. This tyre is used by the Elevron 3.0 "Alt" assembly and fits the photo proportions.
- **The Elevron 3.0 Alt chassis:** 65 × 75.8 × 17 mm.

**Frames and words used throughout:**
- **Robot frame** follows the repository convention:
  - +y is forward, toward the blades and forks.
  - +x is the robot's **right**.
  - +z is up; z = 0 is the axle line and the floor is at z ≈ −10.75.
- **Viewer-left, viewer-right and up-in-image** refer to the photo frame.
- **Near side** is the long side facing the camera. In both photos that is the robot's **right** side (+x).
  - The forks and the blade point to the viewer's right and slightly toward the camera, so the robot faces right-and-toward the viewer.
  - Of its two long sides, the one nearer the camera is therefore its right.
  - Parts higher in the image are further from the camera.
- **[guess]** marks something that cannot be seen and is inferred from symmetry, from the Elevron 3.0 family, or from how such a robot has to work.
- **[ratio]** marks a size read off the photo as a ratio to the tyre diameter (D = 21.5 mm).

**What the robot is.** A 4-wheel-drive antweight (150 g class) **servo lifter**:
- A low black printed tub carries four N10-class gearmotors driving four black tyres.
- The tub is wrapped on both sides by red printed side panels (the wheel guards, or "pods").
- A red printed cover over the servo sits on the right (+x) half of the top.
- A long raked arm runs along the left-centre of the top. It has a black side plate, a red top rail and a red blade tip, and pivots at the rear top.
- A white servo horn and a short link drive the arm.

The two photos show two **front-end configurations**. AWS74 has long red forks on both side panels. AWS72 has short pods ending in a wedge face.

## Section 1: View by view

### 1.1 AWS74 (front-right three-quarter, long forks)

**Composition.** The robot fills the frame diagonally.
- The rear, with the arm pivot, is upper-left: about x = 330–560 px, y = 60–250 px.
- The near fork tip is lower-right at about (1460, 900) px. The far fork tip is at about (1700, 520) px.
- The arm blade tip is at about (1330, 640) px, well behind both fork tips (about 10 mm behind them in robot y).

**From near to far, the camera sees the following.**

1. **Near (right, +x) side panel.** It runs across the bottom half of the image, from the rear pod (lower-left, about 60–330 px wide) to the near fork tip (lower-right). Rear to front:
   - **Rear pod:** red, with a rounded top, and **three vertical slot vents** moulded into its outer face.
   - **Rear wheel opening:** a scalloped dip in the panel's top line between the rear pod and the mid web. The top of the rear tyre shows through it.
   - **Mid web:** a lower section between the wheels. Its **outer vertical face** carries **two black button-head screws** in counterbored recesses:
     - one mid-way between the wheels, inside a larger round recess about 4–5 mm across;
     - one closer to the front wheel and slightly lower.
   - **Front pod:** a taller box behind and around the front wheel, with **five vertical slot vents** on its outer face. Its top edge rounds over toward the inside.
   - **Fork:** forward of the front pod, the panel narrows into a long, flat, tapering fork that runs down to a point on the floor.
     - About 2/3 of the way along its top edge there is a small **saw-tooth step**: the top drops vertically about 1.5–2 mm, then continues at the same slope to the tip.
     - The fork's outer face carries a faint moulded diagonal line, a print seam or chamfer edge [guess].
2. **Black tub.** It shows as a dark band above the near side panel, between the panel and the red cover. The side wall is plain matte black with printed layer lines. Through the gap at the top of the tub, small black parts show under the cover's front edge: the slide switch body, and dark wiring [wiring is a guess].
3. **Red servo cover.** A trapezoidal red block on the right half of the tub.
   - **Screws:** three black button heads.
     - Two are near the front corners, at the lower front of the cover: about (285, 285) px and (600, 385) px.
     - One is at the rear-outer corner, below the stud.
   - **Engraving:** an engraved **"0 1"** on the upper sloped face, reading rear to front.
     - The characters are about 4–6 mm tall [ratio: about 0.25 D].
     - The 0 is a slanted oval and the 1 is a slanted bar.
   - **Stud:** at the rear-outer corner, a **silver hex nut on a threaded steel stud** pointing outward (+x).
4. **White servo horn.** It stands vertically between the cover's inner (−x) edge and the arm's black plate.
   - A small silver screw head sits near its top.
   - Below it, a short silver segment runs toward the arm: a link or pushrod [guess; only a short bright piece shows].
5. **Arm.** It runs from the pivot boss down and forward to the blade tip. The boss is upper-left and is the highest point in the image.
   - A **red anodised button-head screw** sits in the centre of the boss.
   - The near face of the arm is a black plate, with the red top rail above it.
   - A small black **hook/notch** stands on the top edge about 40% of the way from the pivot.
   - A second **red/brown screw head** sits low on the black plate near the front, about 85% along.
6. **Far (left, −x) side panel.**
   - Its front pod shows above and behind the arm, with the far front tyre visible inside it.
   - Its long fork runs to the far tip at the right edge of the image and has the same saw-tooth step.
   - Its inner face shows a large shallow scoop (concave) where it passes the front wheel.
7. **Far tyres.** Only the top of the front-left tyre shows, inside the far front pod. The rear-left tyre is hidden behind the arm and the cover.

### 1.2 AWS72 (front-right three-quarter, a little more side-on, short pods)

**Composition.** The rear is upper-left. The arm blade tip is lower-right at about (1240, 800) px. The near side panel runs along the bottom-left.

**Differences from AWS74:**
- **No forks.** Each side panel ends just ahead of its front wheel in a **steep wedge face**.
  - The near front pod's sloped top and front carry a moulded **triangle outline**: a raised or engraved right-triangle frame, about 10 × 6 mm.
  - The far front pod shows the same triangle on its upper sloped face.
  - The near front pod shows **four** vents ("IIII") instead of five. It may be a different print, or one slot is hidden.
- **The arm is the furthest-forward part.**
  - The red blade tip ends in a **dark steel/brown edge strip** about 4 mm deep across its full width. This is a steel or titanium scoop edge [guess: glued or screwed under the red tip].
  - The black plate ends about 15 mm behind the red tip. It is cut square at the front and has a sloped lower edge.
  - The front of the black plate sits lower than the red rail, so a black band about 8 mm deep shows under the red.
- **Cover screws:** two black screws at the front and one at the rear-outer corner. A small round hole about 1 mm across sits beside each front screw: a locating pin hole or vent [guess].
- **Red-capped screw on the arm:** low on the black arm plate, about 30% along, level with the cover's front edge and just above the black tub.
- **Far (left) side panel:** both of its pods show at the top-right, each with a rounded outer top edge. The far rear tyre is visible.
- **Small red block:** a small red rectangular block, about 6 × 4 × 4 mm, sits on the black tub front, between the near front pod and the arm. It appears to be the inner tab of the near front pod, reaching over the tub's front-right corner.

**Details that are clearer here:**
- The white horn, which has a rounded top.
- The red pivot cap, which is a red domed button head inside a red washer.
- The steel stud and nut. They stick out of the rear-outer corner of the cover/housing, in line with the pivot axis but lower in the image, because they are nearer the camera.
- Through the opening in front of the cover, the black slide switch body: about 12 × 4 mm, with its slider showing.

### 1.3 What neither photo shows

Neither photo shows:

- the outer face of the left side panel;
- the inside of the tub: motors, battery, ESC and wires.

These are assumed:
- The far side panel mirrors the near one. AWS72's far pods, seen from inside, look mirrored.
- The tub layout is the Elevron 3.0 one [guess, from the family]:
  - four N10 gearmotors, gearboxes toward the wheels, terminals inward;
  - battery, receiver/ESC and switch between them.

## Section 2: Part by part

All sizes are approximate. They are derived from the tyre diameter unless the Elevron 3.0 data gives them.

### 2.1 Black chassis tub

- **Shape:** an open-top rectangular box.
  - **Plan:** about 65 mm wide (x) by about 76 mm long (y), with rectangular wheel wells cut into the four corners.
  - **Height:** about 17 mm (0.8 D), centred on the axle line.
  - **Top:** z ≈ 8.5, about level with the tyre tops (8.5 vs 10.75), or a little lower.
  - **Front:** a chamfered ramp edge, the wedge under the arm. AWS74 shows it under the arm, between the forks.
- **Colour:** matte black print with visible layer lines.
- **Position:**
  - Centred between the four wheels, with the side panels outside it on both sides.
  - The red cover sits on its right half. The arm lies over the left-centre of its top at rest.
  - Its side walls show as a black band about 5–7 mm tall, between the side panel web tops and the cover's lower edge.
- **Faces that touch:**
  - **Top face:** carries the cover/housing on the right. The arm rests just above it on the left.
  - **Outer side walls between the wheel wells:** carry the side panels' mid webs, screwed horizontally into them.
  - **Wheel wells:** clear the tyres by about 1–1.5 mm [guess].
- **Holds:** four gearmotors, battery, receiver/ESC and switch. The switch is visible through the top; the rest is a guess from the family.
- **Fasteners:**
  - It takes the side panel screws (2 per side) and the cover/housing screws (3 visible on top).
  - [guess] A separate top plate or lid under the cover is screwed down. The servo has to come out for service, which is why the cover is screwed.

### 2.2 Red servo cover / housing (robot right, +x half of the top)

- **Shape:** a long trapezoidal block.
  - **Base:** about 26 mm wide (x), from the tub's right edge inward to about the centreline. About 50 mm long (y), from the tub rear to about the front axle (y ≈ 20).
  - **Top:** about 15–16 mm above the tub top [ratio: about 0.7 D], so z ≈ 24.
  - **Outer (+x) and inner (−x) walls:** chamfered at about 45° near the top (about 3 mm chamfers), leaving a flat top strip.
  - **Front:** a long slope of about 45°, from the top down to the tub top. It starts about 12 mm behind the front base edge.
  - **Rear:** vertical, flush with the tub's rear face.
  - **Top groove:** a shallow **longitudinal groove** about 1 mm deep runs down the centre of the top. AWS72 shows it as a trough between two raised side rails; AWS74 shows it less clearly.
  - **"0 1":** engraved on the outer sloped face, near the rear-middle.
- **Colour:** red, the same red as the side panels.
- **Position relative to other parts:**
  - Sits on the tub top, flush with the tub's right outer edge and its rear.
  - Its inner edge is about 2.5–3 mm from the arm's black plate. The servo horn stands in that gap.
  - Its front slope ends about 10 mm behind the front axle line.
  - The **pivot lug** (black block, see 2.6) sits at its rear-inner corner.
  - The stud and nut stick out from its rear-outer region at z ≈ 25–28.
- **Fastening:**
  - **Three black M2 button-head screws** go in from the top, into the tub or lid below: front-inner corner, front-outer corner and rear-outer corner.
  - Each sits in a counterbore so the head is nearly flush.
  - [guess] A fourth screw at the rear-inner corner is hidden by the arm boss.
- **Mechanism:**
  - The cover encloses the servo, which lies on its side with the output spline pointing −x toward the arm.
  - The spline comes through the inner wall at about mid-height, about 6 mm from the servo's rear end. That puts it at about y ≈ −10, z ≈ 15.

### 2.3 Servo (black, hidden)

- **Type [guess, from the cover's size]:** a micro servo of about 23 × 12 × 23 mm with mounting ears, SG90/CS07 class.
- **Orientation:** lying flat, output axis along x, pointing at the arm (−x).
- **Visible:**
  - A sliver of black body and a silver screw head behind the horn (AWS72).
  - A silver metal edge under the horn (AWS74, about (560, 230) px), probably the servo case screw or the horn screw.
- **Fastening [guess]:** trapped in a pocket in the housing and closed by the screwed cover. The ears sit in slots, so the servo cannot spin under arm torque.
- **Wires [guess]:** a 3-wire lead leaves the servo's front end and drops through the housing floor into the tub, to the receiver.

### 2.4 White servo horn

- **Shape:** a standard single-arm horn in white nylon.
  - Hub about 7 mm in diameter.
  - Arm about 14 mm long, about 5 mm wide at the hub, tapering to about 3.5 mm, with a rounded end.
  - A small steel screw or pin near the end, which is the link pivot.
- **Position:**
  - Stands **vertically** in the 2.6 mm gap between the cover's inner wall and the arm's black plate.
  - The hub is on the servo spline at y ≈ −10, z ≈ 15.
  - With the arm at rest, the horn tip points **up** (+z) and reaches z ≈ 29, almost level with the pivot boss.
- **Gaps [guess, from the visible slit]:** about 0.4 mm to the cover wall and about 0.4 mm to the arm plate.
- **Mechanism:**
  - The horn drives the arm through a short link, a stiff wire or a pin [guess]; AWS74 shows a short bright segment below the horn.
  - When the servo turns, the horn pushes the link and the link pushes the arm up about the rear pivot.
  - An equally likely reading [guess] is a pin from the horn tip running in a slot in the arm.

### 2.5 Lifter arm (the weapon)

**Overall.** A long raked plate.
- **Length:** about 113 mm (5.3 D), from the pivot (y ≈ −25) to the blade tip. The tip is at y ≈ 88 in AWS74 and further forward in AWS72.
- **Width (x):** about 12 mm.
- **Position:**
  - Left-centre of the robot.
  - Its outer (+x) face is about 2.6 mm left of the cover's inner wall.
  - Its inner (−x) face is at about x = −15.

**Layers.**
- **Black main plate:** the full profile.
- **Red top rail:** about 2.5 mm deep along the whole top edge, the same width as the plate, with slightly chamfered edges.
  - In AWS74 a thin black line shows between the rail and the plate: either the black plate's top edge stands slightly proud of the red, or there is a black lamina.
- **Red blade tip:** about 14–15 mm long at the front, lying flat on the floor.
  - In AWS74 it is squared off, with a small step on its top.
  - In AWS72 it carries a dark steel/brown edge strip about 4 mm deep.

**Profile (side view, y–z).**
- **Boss:** a round boss about 16 mm in diameter around the pivot at (y −25, z 30). It is the highest point of the robot.
- **Top edge:**
  - From the top of the boss it runs forward almost level, falling about 3–5 mm over the first 40%.
  - At about 40% there is a **step**. A small black **hook** stands about 2 mm proud and points back, and the top drops about 2.5 mm.
  - After the step it continues as a straight rake down to the blade tip on the floor.
- **Black plate depth:** behind the step the plate is deep, about 12 mm under the rail.
- **Bottom edge:**
  - Runs nearly straight from under the boss down to just above the tub top (about 1 mm clear).
  - Then follows the tub's front ramp.
  - Ahead of the tub, the black plate ends in a sloped lower edge; only the red rail continues to the tip. In AWS72 the black plate is about 8 mm deep at its front end.

**Screws in the arm.**
- **Pivot screw:** one **red anodised M3 button head** in the boss.
- **Other red/brown button head:** low on the outer black face, at about 30% along (AWS72) or about 85% along (AWS74).
  - These may be two different screws, and both may be present [guess]. They are probably plate-lamination bolts.
- **Black screw end at the step:** AWS74 shows one. The hook may be the head of a screw [guess].

**Mechanism.**
- The arm rotates about the x-axis through the pivot.
- At rest the tip is on the floor. Driven up by the servo, the tip rises through about 60° [guess].
- At rest the arm lies **over** the tub top with about 1 mm clearance, and about 2.6 mm from the cover. Its front underside follows the tub's front ramp.

### 2.6 Pivot lug, pivot screw, red cap, stud and nut

**Pivot lug.** A **black block** at the rear-inner corner of the cover, between the cover and the arm boss. It reads black in both photos, as part of the black lug/plate system.
- About 4 mm thick (x).
- Rises from the cover rear up to the pivot height.
- Its upper edge is rounded around the pivot, with an arched shape toward the front.

**Pivot screw.** An **M3 button head**, red anodised or seated on a red aluminium washer.
- It goes in from the arm's inner (−x) face, through the arm boss and then through the lug.

**Stud and nut.**
- Past the lug, about 4–6 mm of threaded stud sticks out, with a **silver M3 hex nut** tightened against the lug's outer face. The nut is 5.5 mm across flats and about 1.8–2.4 mm thick.
- This is the "threaded stud" seen above the rear-outer area of the cover.
- In AWS72 the stud points toward the viewer-left, out of the robot's right side, at about z ≈ 26–28. It appears slightly below the red cap in the image.
- That fits one straight bolt along x: the pivot is at z ≈ 30, and the stud is nearer the camera.
- If the stud is **not** on the pivot axis, it is a separate cross bolt holding the lug to the cover [guess, less likely].

**Gaps and motion.**
- The arm boss has a clearance hole on the M3, about 0.2 mm.
- The lug hole is a tight fit.
- The red cap stops the arm moving outward along the axis.

### 2.7 Side panels / wheel guards (red; one per side, mirrored)

**Each panel is one red printed piece** that wraps the outside of both wheels on its side. It may be TPU or PLA [guess: flexible pods are common on Elevrons].

**Plan, rear to front.**

1. **Rear pod**
   - Behind and outside the rear wheel.
   - About 19 mm wide (x, from about x = 33 to x = 52) and about 25 mm long (y, from about −37 to −12).
   - **Top:** about z = 7, below the tyre top.
   - **Outer top edges:** well **rounded**, about R3.
   - **Outer face:** **three vertical slot vents**, each about 1.5 mm wide, 0.6 mm deep and 8 mm tall.
   - **Wheel side:** an arched opening around the rear tyre, with about 1.25 mm clearance.
2. **Mid web** (between the wheels, |y| < 14)
   - A plate at about axle-top height.
   - **Top:** about z = 7. The top line dips in a **scallop** between the rear and front wheels (AWS72, AWS74).
   - **Outer face:** vertical, carrying **two black M2 button-head screws** in counterbored recesses.
     - One is near the middle, in a larger round recess (about 4.5 mm).
     - One is just behind the front wheel.
   - The screws run **horizontally (−x)** into the black tub's side wall, or into a boss on it [the boss is a guess].
   - **Inner edge:** against the tub side wall (contact, about 0.2 mm).
3. **Front pod**
   - Wraps the front and outside of the front wheel.
   - About 19 mm wide. **Top:** about z = 9, taller than the rear pod and level with the tyre top.
   - **Outer top edge:** rounded.
   - **Outer face:** five slot vents in AWS74, four in AWS72.
   - **Inner front corner:** a small square **tab** reaches inward over the tub's front corner. In AWS72 it is the red block; in AWS74 it is a small red box with a slot, next to the arm front.
4. **Front end in AWS74: the fork**
   - Starts at the front pod (y ≈ 40) about 9 mm wide and tapers to about 3.5 mm at the tip. The tip is at y ≈ 98.6, from the Alt side panel data.
   - The top slopes from about z = 9 down to the floor.
   - About 12 mm before the tip there is a **saw-tooth step**: a vertical drop of about 1.5 mm, then a flat blade about 1.6 mm thick to the tip.
   - The inner edge is straight, at about x = 43.
5. **Front end in AWS72: the short pod**
   - The front pod ends at about y ≈ 50 in a steep sloped wedge face down to the floor.
   - The moulded triangle frame sits on that face.

**Contacts and fastening.**
- **Underside:** open. The panels are hollow shells about 1.6 mm thick [guess: printed top-down].
- **Fastening:**
  - 2 horizontal M2 screws per side, through the outer face of the mid web into the tub side (the screw heads are visible).
  - The front pod's inner tab may hook over the tub front [guess].
- **Floor clearance:** about 0.5 mm. The pods and forks skim the floor; the fork tips touch it.

### 2.8 Wheels and hubs

- **Count:** four.
- **Tyres:** black rubber or foam, about 21.5 mm OD and 9.5 mm wide.
- **Hubs:** black or grey, about 11 mm across.
- **Stations:** x = ±37, y = ±26 (Alt data). This matches the photo spacing: the front and rear tyres are about 2.4 D apart, centre to centre.
- **Height:** the tyre tops are about 0–2 mm above the tub top and below the front pod top. They show as black discs inside the pod openings.
- **Hub detail:** in AWS72 the rear-left tyre shows a dark hub centre with a screw or shaft end [the shaft end].
- **Fastening [guess]:** the hubs are D-bored and pressed onto the 3 mm gearmotor shafts; the tyres are stretched onto the hubs.
- **Gaps [guess, from the visible slits]:** about 1.25 mm from tyre to the panel walls and to the tub wheel wells.

### 2.9 Drive motors (hidden)

- **Type:** four N10 micro-metal gearmotors, 12 × 10 mm section, about 33 mm long including the shaft. Inferred from the Elevron family and the hub size.
- **Placement:**
  - In the tub, along x, at y = ±26.
  - Gearboxes outboard; shafts pass through the tub walls into the hubs.
  - Terminal ends inboard.
- **Fastening [guess]:** sandwiched between the tub and its top plate/lid, in printed cradles. Elevron 3.0 uses a clamping top.

### 2.10 Electronics and wires (hidden except for the switch)

- **Switch:** a black slide switch.
  - The body and lever are visible through the black top, just ahead of the cover and to its left: AWS72 at about (360, 360) px, and AWS74 at about (400, 360) px under the cover's front-left.
  - It sits inside the tub near the front-centre and is reached from the top or from the belly.
- **Battery [guess]:** a 2S LiPo of about 180 mAh (28 × 18 × 10 mm). It lies flat on the tub floor along y, on one side: left of centre, under the arm.
- **Receiver/ESC [guess]:** Malenki Nano class, about 23 × 16 × 5 mm, on the other side of the floor.
- **Wires [guess]:**
  - **Battery to switch to ESC:** about 22 AWG, red and black.
  - **ESC to motors:** four pairs of about 26 AWG along the floor, to the inboard motor terminals.
  - **ESC to servo:** a 3-wire 28 AWG lead, up through the lid into the servo bay.

## Section 4: Contacts and gaps between parts

"Touch" means the faces are in contact. A number is the gap in mm. Pairs not listed are not adjacent.

**Tub:**
- **Cover:** touches; the cover's bottom sits on the tub top and is screwed down.
- **Lug:** touches at its bottom.
- **Arm:** 1.0, from the arm underside to the tub top at rest.
- **Right side panel:** touches; the web's inner edge meets the tub side wall, with 2 screws.
- **Left side panel:** touches, mirroring the right.
- **Tyres:** 1.25 in the wheel wells.

## Section 5: Mechanisms

1. **Drive.**
   - Four N10 gearmotors, each driving its own tyre directly, with no belts. Skid steering.
   - The left pair and the right pair run on two ESC channels [guess].
2. **Weapon.**
   - The servo, whose output is along x, turns the white horn.
   - The horn's tip drives the arm through a short link, or a pin in a slot [guess], raising the arm about its rear pivot.
   - The front blade gets under the opponent and the arm lifts it. The blade is the red tip, with a steel edge in AWS72.
   - The drive point is close to the pivot (about 15 mm) and the tip about 113 mm away. That makes the arm a third-class lever: a fast, low-force flipper/lifter.
3. **Wedges.**
   - In AWS74 the two long red forks lead and the arm sits behind them. In AWS72 the arm blade leads.
   - The tub's front ramp and the fronts of the side pods are the secondary wedges.
4. **Service.**
   - The cover comes off with 3 screws, giving access to the servo.
   - The tub top comes off for the electronics [guess].
   - Each side panel comes off with 2 screws.
   - The arm comes off by undoing the pivot nut.

## Section 12 — Consistency check (conflicts and reconciled values)

1. **Cover side: robot right or left?** Photo geometry (near side = robot right, cover on near side) says **right (+x)**. Older model comments (`params.py` docstring "+x is the robot's left") conflict. Reconciled: cover/housing at **+x = robot right** (repository rule "right is +x"); the params docstring is wrong and is corrected.
2. **Front end.** AWS74 forks vs AWS72 short pods. Reconciled: **AWS74 forks**, because the SolidWorks Alt side panel (tip y 98.6) matches it; AWS72 recorded as a different build.
3. **Side-panel screw direction.** Photos show heads on the **outer vertical face** of the web (horizontal screws, −x). The current CAD round 6 puts 2 M2 screws **down through the web top** into chassis flank blocks. Conflict: open. Reconciled target: horizontal screws through the outer face (same count, 2 per side); the vertical version is an interim that holds the part equally.
4. **Vent count, front pod.** AWS74 five, AWS72 four. Reconciled: **five** (follows the AWS74 configuration).
5. **Red screw on the arm side.** AWS72 at ≈ 30 % along, AWS74 at ≈ 85 %. Reconciled: two separate lamination screws, both present [guess]; not modelled yet.
6. **Stud on the pivot axis?** In both photos the stud appears 4–8 mm "lower" than the pivot cap; perspective (stud ≈ 25 mm nearer the camera along +x, elevation 35°) predicts an apparent drop of ≈ 25 · sin(35°)·(projection) ≈ 6–10 mm in image terms. Reconciled: **same axis**, one M3 × 25 screw with nut.
7. **Pivot height.** Photo ratio gives 37–41 mm above the floor; design 40.75. Reconciled: **z = 30** (40.75 above floor).
8. **Arm length.** Pixel estimate 115–125 vs design 113 (AWS74 tip at y 88). Reconciled: **113**; the pixel figure carries ±10 % perspective error.
9. **Fork length.** Alt data gives tip y 98.6 (43 mm past the front pod at y 55.6); pixel estimate suggests ≈ 50–60 mm. Reconciled: **Alt data (y 98.6)**; the pod front y may be ≈ 5 mm further back in reality.

11. **Lid existence.** Not visible; required for the electronics to be serviceable and for the motors to be clamped. Reconciled: **separate 1.6 mm black lid** screwed with 3 M2 [guess], under the red housing.
12. **Material of side panels.** Could be PLA (glossy layer lines in AWS74) or TPU. Reconciled: **TPU** (Elevron pods; impact parts), flagged as a guess.
13. **Horn drive.** Link vs pin-in-slot. Reconciled: horn **press-fit on the spline**, arm on its own pivot; the link is not modelled [guess].
