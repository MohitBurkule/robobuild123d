# Boss Baby: reference description

Photos: `reference/boss_baby.aws72.webp` (the main one: black version, white background, camera at front-right and above, about az 60 / el 35) and `reference/boss_baby.aws73.webp` (an older white-bodied build on a pallet, camera at front-left and above; same layout, used only to read the mechanism).
Model axes: +y is the front (where the forks point), +x is the robot's right. In aws72 the robot faces the camera, so the IMAGE-LEFT pod is the robot's RIGHT (+x).

## Overall layout
- Two separate rectangular pods side by side, with an open slot about 20-26 mm wide between them. Total width is about 135-145 mm, depth (y) about 55-60 mm per pod, and height about 22-24 mm. The pods are flat-topped boxes with sharp vertical corners and small chamfers on the top edges. They are roughly square in plan, a little wider (x) than deep or about equal.
- A vertical spinner sits in the centre slot: a direct-drive outrunner (red bell) on a lateral (x) axis, with a thin ring disc bolted to the bell face. The disc sits just inboard of the LEFT (-x) pod, and the motor body fills most of the slot.
- 4 steel fork tines, 2 per pod, hang from small black clevis brackets on the lower front face of each pod and slope down to the floor ahead of the robot.
- There are 2 wheels, inside the pods (not outboard). Each shows through a rectangular slot in the top plate near the OUTER-REAR corner of its pod. The wheels are small (about 20-24 mm diameter) with black treaded tyres and sit about flush with the top plate. No wheel shows on the pod side faces.

## Pods (black FDM nylon/PLA, colour ~#1c1d20)
- Each pod is an open tub plus a separate top plate. The top plate's edge shows as a seam about 3 mm below the top surface, and the plate is held by small silver Phillips/button screws: about 4-5 per pod, at the corners and the mid front edge.
- RIGHT pod (+x, image-left): its top plate reaches inboard past the slot at the REAR. Behind the motor it becomes a deck at the same height as the pod top, with a concave curved front edge round the motor. This makes the right pod's top an L shape (wider at the back). A black printed motor-mount block (the stator mount) stands at the inboard edge of this pod, holding the motor's stator face with 4 screws (seen in aws73: a white boss plate with 4 black cap screws). The battery lead (red/black wires to a red XT30/JST plug in a black shrink-wrapped lead) leaves through a hole in the rear-inboard part of this top plate and sticks up loose. That lead is a cable, not structure.
- LEFT pod (-x, image-right): a plain rectangle. A dark upright behind the disc at the inboard edge (the second shaft support / bearing block) is partly visible through the ring. A white 3-pin JST balance/servo plug lies on its top near the disc (loose wiring).
- Lower front face of each pod: a slightly recessed band where the fork brackets bolt on (two screw heads per bracket).

## Weapon
- A ring disc: steel, silver, about 55-60 mm OD and about 3 mm thick. The outer rim band is only about 4-6 mm wide, and the centre is open or dark (the black bell end / hub shows through), so it reads as a ring. It has 2 small tooth steps on the rim (visible bottom-right in aws72).
- Axis on x at about 18-20 mm above the axle line. The disc top stands about 25-30 mm above the pod tops, and the bottom comes close to the floor (a few mm above). Fore-aft, the axis is near the pod mid-depth or slightly forward, so the disc front edge is about level with the pod front faces (it does not lead the forks).
- The motor is an outrunner of roughly 2204/1806 size, about 23-25 mm diameter and 20 mm long. It has a red anodised bell with black end rings, and its stator face is bolted to the right pod's mount block. The disc is on the bell's -x face. The shaft continues into the left pod's support (motor-as-hub, supported both sides).
- Lock pin: none visible in the photo; it will be modelled as a pin hole through both uprights and the disc.

## Forks / wedge
- 4 narrow steel tines (about 4-5 mm wide, 1-1.5 mm thick, about 45-50 mm long), tapering to points with a small step near the tip. They sit in pairs close together (about 10-12 mm apart) near the MIDDLE of each pod's front face, not at the outer edges.
- Each tine root is clamped in a black printed clevis bracket (U-shaped, two cheeks, a cross bolt) on the pod front lower face. They may be hinged, resting on the floor. They drop at about 15-20 deg so the tips touch or nearly touch the floor about 40 mm ahead of the pods. In aws73 the forks are wider flat blades on long black side rails (the older version).

## Wheels / drive
- 2WD, wheels inside the pods near the rear-outer corners. The drive gearmotors (N10/N20 class) are inside the pods, with axles along x. The wheels touch the floor through the floor openings.

## Relative sizes
- Pod height : disc diameter is about 1 : 2.4. The pod is about the same width as the disc diameter.
- Motor diameter is about the pod height. The slot width is about the motor length.
- Fork length is about 0.8 x the pod depth.

## Colours
Pods, lids and brackets are black. The disc and forks are bare steel. The motor bell is red with black ends. The wheels are black. Wiring is red/black with a red plug and a white JST plug.
