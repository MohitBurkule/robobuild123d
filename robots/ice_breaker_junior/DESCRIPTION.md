# Ice Breaker Junior: reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/ice_breaker_junior.aws74.webp`, `...aws76.webp`, `...aws77.webp` (AWS wiki, cut out; a green colour cast appears on black surfaces in AWS74/76).

Robot type: **front-mounted vertical spinner (small disc/drum)** with **two long front forks**, and **two-wheel drive** at the rear. Almost everything is **black PLA**. The accents are **blue** rear wheel covers and wheel hubs, and **translucent grey knurled tyres**. Two **black servo horns** stick up from the top middle: servos for weapon tilt or a self-righter/arm *(guess; see Mechanism)*.

Conventions: **front** = the forks/disc end. **W** = body width across the rear wheel covers. Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Main parts
1. **Body / chassis**: a black printed box about 0.8 W wide (between the wheel covers) × 0.9 W long × 0.25 W tall. It has a flat **black top lid** (about 0.55 W × 0.5 W) at the rear-left half, with **4 silver Phillips screws** at the corners. A raised **central hump/tunnel** (about 0.3 W wide, 0.08 W higher than the lid) runs fore–aft down the middle, with a **rectangular window** in its top (servo or switch access).
2. **Rear wheels (×2)**: at the two rear corners. Each is Ø about 0.33 W and 0.12 W wide. The **tyres are translucent grey/white TPU with a coarse knurl / gear-tooth tread**, on blue hubs. Each wheel's **outer side is covered by a blue (left) or blue/black (right) printed cover**: a "D"/rounded-teardrop plate, about 0.4 W tall, bolted to a **blue or black bracket arm** that extends from the body side. The bracket has **2 × 2 lightening holes** and a screw (AWS74/77 left cover is blue, AWS76 right cover is black). The covers act as hub caps / side guards. The wheels stick up above the lid by about 0.1 W.
3. **Front forks (×2)**: two **long black printed forks** (tapered flat bars), one at each front corner, angling down and forward from the body front corners to the floor. Each is about 0.6–0.7 W long, 0.1 W wide at the root tapering to 0.07 W, and about 0.03 W thick at the tip. They have a **chisel tip**. At the root each has a **gusset/fillet** joining the fork to a **front corner block** (about 0.2 W cube) on the body. The forks are roughly parallel, about 0.75 W apart (outer edges), and point straight forward.
4. **Weapon (disc/drum)**: a **steel disc**, about 0.35 W in diameter and 0.05 W thick, with a **bright silver rim**, a dark face and a central hub/bolt. It spins on a **transverse (left–right) axle** in a **vertical plane parallel to the direction of travel**, so it is a vertical spinner. It sits at the **front**, **just right of centre** (AWS74). On its left a **black outrunner motor** (with orange/red wire windings visible) directly drives it. The assembly is carried between **two black printed bearing/motor blocks** (yokes), each about 0.12 W thick, with their own screws. The weapon's lower edge is roughly at fork-root height, and its top is level with the body top.
5. **Weapon support arm / plate**: a black **pointed hexagonal "beak" plate** (about 0.25 W across) with a **round hole** lies flat on the front top of the body, pointing toward the disc, with silver screws. It looks like the end of an arm that carries the weapon yokes. The yokes are linked to it by 2–3 Phillips screws.
6. **Servo horns (×2)**: two **black single-arm servo horns**, standing **upright** (about 0.2 W tall), at the left and right ends of the central hump, just behind the weapon. Each has a **silver screw/hex** at its hub and a hole near its tip. A **black S-shaped/curved link plate** with **3 Phillips screws** (AWS74/77) lies flat on the left beside the hump.
7. **Blue jumper / connector**: a small **blue rectangular jumper or link** next to the left servo (AWS74), likely the power link *(guess)*.
8. **Clear LED/lens**: a small **clear rectangular part** on the right of the top (AWS76), likely a power-indicator LED *(guess)*.

## Mechanism (inferred, uncertain)
- **Drive**: 2 rear wheels plus fork skids (2WD). The motors are inside the body behind the wheels *(guess)*.
- **Weapon**: an outrunner direct-drives a small steel disc (an "eggbeater-like" drum), mounted on the front of the body between the forks.
- **Servo horns**: probably two servos rotating a **weapon-carrying front arm** (the beak plate plus yokes) up and down, to tilt the spinner or work as a lifter *(guess)*. The S-shaped plate with 3 screws may be a linkage or horn adaptor. Alternatively the servos are the **drive** (continuous-rotation servos driving the rear wheels through the hubs), but their horns are on the top, so a weapon-tilt function is more likely.

## Hidden / uncertain
- The servo function, the hinge/pivot of the weapon arm, and the drive motors.
- The battery, ESC and receiver are inside the body.
- Disc form: it may be a small drum or a two-tooth disc. Only the rim is seen.
