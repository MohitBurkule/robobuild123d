# Slipper: reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/slipper.aws62.webp`, `slipper.aws63.webp`, `slipper.aws64.webp`. All three are **small, low-resolution, uncut** photos (about 300–400 px), so details are uncertain.

Robot type: a **full-body wedge** (doorstop shape) with a **hinged top flap** that is likely a **servo flipper/lifter**, or possibly an access lid *(guess)*. The body is **silver/grey**: grey-painted or printed in AWS62/63, folded aluminium sheet in AWS64. The name "Slipper" is hand-written on the side in black marker.

Conventions: **front** = the low, thin edge of the wedge. **Rear** = the tall end. **W** = body width. Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Common form
- **Side profile**: a right-angle-ish triangle/trapezoid. The bottom is flat. The **top face slopes** from a floor-level front edge up to the rear at about 25–30°. The rear is a short vertical (or rounded) wall about 0.35–0.4 W tall. Length is about 1.3–1.4 W.
- **Plan**: rectangular, about W wide × 1.35 W long.
- **Side walls**: flat, solid, with "Slipper" written on the left side.

## Image 1: `slipper.aws62.webp` (AWS 62)
- View: from above-front, steeply down the sloped face, with the robot on a wooden table.
- **Body**: **matt grey/silver** (painted plastic or printed), with slightly rounded edges. The sides are thick (about 0.08 W walls) and flare outward at the bottom.
- **Top flap**: a **perforated grey plate** lying in the sloped top, inset between the side walls. It is about 0.7 W wide × 1.05 W long, with a **5 × 4 grid of square holes** (each about 0.07 W, in two column pairs separated by a solid centre strip). At the **front (low) end**, **2 slotted round-head screws** sit near the corners, plus a small centre screw. These are the hinge or fixing points.
- **Black bar**: across the **top rear edge** is a **black rubber-covered roll/bar**, about 0.7 W long and Ø about 0.1 W (a foam or rubber tube, or the hinge barrel). It sits above the flap's rear end.
- **Rear**: the top-rear has a **rounded corner**. A small round hole sits at the top-left rear (an axle end or switch).

## Image 2: `slipper.aws63.webp` (AWS 63)
- View: from the front-left, elevated, inside a clear plastic tub.
- The **flap is raised/open**. The perforated plate (same 5 × 4 square-hole grid) is lifted at its **rear end**, with the black rubber bar on that rear edge, by about 25–30° above the body. Its **front end stays near the front of the body**, so the flap **pivots about the front (low) edge**, or is held by a linkage. Under the flap you can see:
  - a **white rectangular block** (likely a **servo**, about 0.25 W);
  - **red wires** and a **black/white component** (a switch or receiver);
  - a **perforated base plate** (a grid of square holes, the same pattern).
- The left side wall is grey with **"SLIPPER"** written diagonally. Small holes (screws) show at the top-left rear.
- This strongly suggests a **front-hinged lid lifted by a servo at the rear**, which works as a **rear-acting flipper / self-righter**, *or* the lid is simply opened for access *(uncertain)*.

## Image 3: `slipper.aws64.webp` (AWS 64)
- View: from above-left, on a wooden board.
- A **rebuilt body in bare aluminium sheet**: a flat **top plate** (about 0.95 W × 1.0 W) with **7 countersunk/Phillips screws** scattered (corners plus centre), and the left front corner **folded down** to a sloping front lip.
- At the **right end** (rear) the sheet is **rolled into a rounded cylinder** (Ø about 0.35 W) with **3 screws** along it and a **hinge pin line** (a visible narrow strip/knuckle) between it and the top plate. So a **rear hinged section** (flap or flipper) is mounted on a **piano-hinge-like strip**.
- The side wall shows **"Slipper"** written in black marker.
- The side and underside are folded aluminium.

## Hidden / uncertain
- **Wheels**: none visible in any photo. They must be under the body or recessed in the sides *(guess: two small wheels near the rear underneath)*.
- **Weapon**: the flap is most likely a servo-driven flipper hinged at the front. The rear roll in AWS64 may be the flipper or a self-righting bumper.
- Materials: AWS62/63 grey may be painted wood/plastic or aluminium.
- The resolution is too low for exact dimensions.
