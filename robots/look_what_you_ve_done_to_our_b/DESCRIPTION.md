# Look What You've Done To Our Breakfast: reference description

Photos: aws68 (front view, low elevation, cluttered, indoor), aws71 (front-right iso, cut out, blue cast), aws74 (front-left iso, cut out, true colours).

## Overall
- 4-wheel roller "brick" with a wedge at BOTH ends: front and back are identical, so it drives either way. Plan view is a two-way arrow / flattened hexagon: narrow centre body, full-width end blocks, ends taper inward to a straight tip edge.
- Mirror-symmetric left-right AND front-back about the midpoint between the two wheel pairs.
- Proportions (aws74): length ~ 3.3 wheel diameters (~100 mm); overall width incl. wheels ~ length; body height ~ wheel diameter (~28 mm), wheels poke just above the top plate.

## Chassis (blue FDM print, PLA/nylon, colour ~#2f8fe0)
- Centre section: rectangular box between the wheel pairs, sides set in so the wheels sit in side notches (wheels outboard of the body side, inboard of the end blocks).
- End blocks (x2, identical): full width (flush with or slightly past the wheel outer faces). Vertical back face toward the wheels; top edge level with the top plate; ramp drops from the top edge to a thin lip near the floor (ramp ~50-55 deg, run ~ 3/4 of body height).
- Ramp side cheeks are blue triangles whose outer faces are angled inward in plan (the arrow chamfers), so the tip edge is ~70% of full width (aws68 front view clearly shows the chamfered corners).
- Ramp face: a clear polycarbonate / transparent tape strip covers most of the ramp width (aws74 front, aws71 front; aws68 shows white tape). Strip is flat, glued/taped on the blue ramp; tip edge is the strip edge.
- Wheel wells: centre box sides; small gap between each wheel and the end block.

## Top plate (same blue, slightly lighter, flat)
- Flat lid covering the whole top of centre section and running onto the end-block tops; held by small black/silver countersunk or button-head screws: two rows near each long side (aws74 shows 4-5 per side incl. one near each end), plus a couple on the centreline (aws68: 4 corner screws).
- A small rectangular slot near one end on the centreline: the power switch / link access (aws74, near front-left).
- Marker scribble on the top (cosmetic).

## Wheels (x4)
- Two per side, close together (gap ~3-5 mm between tyres), centred on the robot's length midpoint.
- Diameter ~28-30 mm, width ~12 mm; black foam/rubber tyre on a red 3-spoke plastic hub (aws74: red hub, three curved spokes, central hex/D boss; aws68: red rims with black tyres).
- Directly driven by N20-style gearmotors inside the centre box (one per wheel, axle sideways); hubs on motor shafts, no visible axles or bearings outboard.
- Tyres extend slightly above the top plate and are the lowest point; the wedge lips sit just above the floor.

## Internals (not visible; inferred)
- 4 N20 gearmotors across the centre box, LiPo + combined receiver/ESC (e.g. Malenki) under the lid, switch under the slot.

## Relative sizes
- End block length (plan) ~ 1 wheel diameter; centre section ~ 2 wheel diameters (holds the two wheel pairs).
- Body height ~ wheel diameter; top plate thickness ~2 mm.
- Tip edge width ~ 70% of max width; centre box width ~ 75% of max width.
