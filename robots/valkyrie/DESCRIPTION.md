# Valkyrie: reference description

Photos:
- `aws71`: high-key cut-out, front right, about 35 deg elevation. The drum is across the lower right and the left wall reads "VALKYRIE".
- `aws74`: cut-out with the same layout and lighting, cleaner, with drum windows visible.
- `aws70`: small arena-table shot from above at the front. Not used for masks.

In aws71/74 the robot's front (drum) runs along the lower right. The near side wall carries "VALKYRIE", and the chamfered rear corners are at the upper left.

Frame used below: front +y, right +x, up +z.

## Overall
- This is a compact, low, wide two-wheel drum spinner. The plan is about as long as it is wide or slightly shorter: the width is about 100-105 mm, and the length from the rear wall to the drum front is about 80-90 mm. In the photos it reads much squatter than the current model.
- The height is about 20-25 mm to the top plate. The top plate is the highest surface. The drum sits below it.
- Colours:
  - Body/shell: sea-green anodised or painted folded aluminium (about #2fa77a).
  - Top: clear, slightly frosted polycarbonate. Everything inside shows through it, tinted green-grey.
  - Drum: raw machined aluminium (silver).
  - Drum bearings/hubs: anodised blue (about #2146c7).
  - Inside: red battery leads and black tyres show through the lid. Two white foam blocks sit on top of the components.

## Shell (green aluminium, folded sheet, about 1 mm)
- A single sheet folded into a U/tub. The **side walls** are continuous from the rear corner to the front cheek, with no cut-outs for the wheels. The wheels are fully inside the walls.
  - The wall height is the body height. The top edge meets the clear lid.
  - "VALKYRIE" is engraved/printed in black block capitals on the outer face of the left wall (aws71 shows it on the near wall), centred near mid-length.
- **Rear**: a full-width rear wall. Both rear corners are chamfered at 45 deg, about 12-15 mm legs. The wall follows the chamfer (folded) and the lid has the same chamfered outline.
- **Front cheeks**: the side walls continue forward past the body front as drum guards, one on each side. Shape:
  - The top edge steps down ahead of the body and then slopes down to the front.
  - Each cheek ends in a vertical front edge and a flat foot that rests on the ground.
  - Two black rivets/screws per cheek sit near the front.
  - A clear plastic (polycarbonate) wedge/skid is riveted to the inside/bottom of each cheek tip. It reaches the ground ahead of the drum.
- **Front wall**: across the body front behind the drum, a lower clear or aluminium face. Two small bent aluminium tabs/brackets stand up on the front edge of the lid. They are lid-retaining clips or the drum-motor mount, at about 1/3 and 2/3 of the width.

## Top plate (clear polycarbonate)
- A flat sheet covering the whole body between the walls. It sits on top of the walls with a small overhang.
- Outline: a rectangle with the two rear corners chamfered. The front edge is straight, behind the drum.
- Three or four silver button-head screws hold it down. Two sit at the rear chamfer corners (one per side) and one or two near the front. aws71 shows screws at the rear-left and rear-right chamfers. No notches.

## Internals seen through the lid
- The battery, with red leads, is at the rear left. The ESC/receiver (green PCB) is at the rear centre.
- Two square white foam pads, about 12 mm square, sit at the left-centre and right-centre on top of the drive motors.
- A brass/gold motor (the drum motor, outrunner) is in the centre-front.

## Wheels (2)
- Black tyres with a fine tread (knurled rubber), about 26-28 mm in diameter and about 10 mm wide.
- They sit inside the side walls, just inboard of each wall, at about 40-50 % of the body length from the rear (mid-body), not at the extreme rear.
- The tyres stick up close to the lid underside and stick down below the shell bottom to drive.

## Drum weapon
- A rectangular-section aluminium bar drum (about 12-14 mm square section), not a round drum. It spans almost the full width between the cheeks, about 0.75-0.8 of the body width.
- Two impact edges.
- The face shows four rectangular through-windows (lightening/balance pockets) in a row (aws74), and several black countersunk/cap screws in pairs along it (aws71). These are probably the screws that clamp the bar to the shaft/outrunner.
- Ends: blue anodised bearing housings/collars, about 12 mm diameter and about 6 mm wide, at both ends. They are carried by the cheeks or by the inner tabs. The axis is along x, low, near the ground, just ahead of the body front wall and behind the cheek tips.
- Likely mechanism: belt- or direct-driven by the brushless motor inside (gold can), with ball bearings in the blue housings.

## Relative sizes
- The drum length is about 0.77 of the body width. The drum section is about 0.5 of the wheel diameter.
- The cheeks stick out about 15-20 mm ahead of the body front wall and reach slightly past the drum front.
- The body height is about 0.8-0.9 of the wheel diameter. The walls are about 1 mm thick.

## Notes for the model
- The current model places the wheels outside the walls in notches, makes the body too long behind the drum, and uses an opaque top plate with notches. Fixes:
  - Put the wheels inside the walls.
  - Make the walls continuous.
  - Chamfer the rear corners on both the walls and the plate.
  - Give the plate no notches, with a pale clear colour.
  - Shorten the rear section.
- The mass is over 150 g, so cutting length also helps the mass.
