# Baphomet: what the reference photos show

Photos: `reference/baphomet.aws74.webp` (front-left, low), `baphomet.aws76.webp` (front-left, high, almost plan),
`baphomet.aws77.webp` (front-right, low). Model frame: front +y, right +x, up +z. "Robot's right" = the side on the
viewer's left in aws74/aws76 (front-left views) and the near side in aws77.

## Overall
- Two-wheel horizontal-axis **drum** spinner. A flat, open-topped black tub with chamfered corners; the drum sits fully
  **in front of** the tub between two forward arms; two long, thin metal **spikes** stick forward and down from under
  the arms; a red/black **carbon top plate** with a pentagram sits on top between the wheels.
- Plan proportions (from aws76): tub about 1.2x as wide as it is long; arms + drum add about 35-40 % of the tub length
  in front; spike tips reach about as far again (overall length about 1.4-1.5x the tub length).
- Height: tub walls about 0.25x the tub width; wheel tops stand a few mm above the wall tops; the top plate sits at
  about wall-top height; the arms rise above the plate level at the front (horn-like tops above the drum).
- Colours: tub, arms, drum body matte black FDM (visible layer lines); wheels grey textured TPU; plate red/black
  twill carbon; spikes and drum hooks bare silver metal; the drum motor end bright aluminium; lock/emblem details below.

## Tub (chassis)
- Shape: octagon in plan: long straight side walls, straight rear wall, rear corners chamfered at about 45 deg
  (chamfer leg about 12-15 % of the width); front corners also chamfered (aws76 near side, aws77 near side), the
  chamfer faces carry the arm roots.
- Walls: thin (about 2-3 mm), full height (about 22-25 mm), vertical. The side walls are flat outside with 3-4 small
  black screw heads in a row near the top and bottom edges (aws77 left: two rows on the side and the rear chamfer).
  The side walls cover the wheels from the outside: the wheels are inboard of the outer walls, in wells.
- Open top: the inside is visible around the plate: wheel wells on both sides, a black inner rail frame (aws76: a
  rectangular printed frame under the plate edges, its front rail a raised lip just behind the drum), motor gearboxes
  under the plate (brass pinion visible at the far wheel in aws76).
- Floor: not visible; the tub sits a few mm off the ground.

## Wheels (2, driven)
- One per side, at about the middle of the tub length (in line with the plate centre), inside the side walls.
- Grey TPU tyres with fine axial tread ribs all round (gear-like), about 30-35 mm diameter, about 12 mm wide; black hub
  with a recessed face/spokes (aws74 right wheel shows dark spokes). Wheel tops protrude above the side walls by about
  20-30 % of the diameter.
- Mechanism: direct drive from N20 / micro-metal gearmotors lying across the robot under the plate (gearbox at the
  wheel side).

## Top plate
- Red/black twill carbon fibre plate, about 60 % of the tub width and about 70 % of its length, centred left-right,
  set slightly rearward of the tub centre (the front rail and drum are ahead of it).
- 4 silver button/socket screws (M2, with washers/nylon nuts) near its corners, inset about 8-10 mm.
- A rectangular switch hole (about 7 x 5 mm) near the rear, offset to the robot's right; a black slide switch visible
  in it; an orange-red "ON | OFF" label sticker beside it toward the right rear corner.
- Pentagram emblem centred on the plate (slightly forward of centre): black disc (about 40 % of plate width), thin
  white/silver outer ring, red line-art inverted pentagram (one point toward the rear) with a goat head inside.
- Held: 4 screws into the tub frame posts; rests on the inner rail frame.

## Arms (2, separate black prints)
- Curved crescent/boomerang plates, about 5 mm thick (x), one each side, rooted on the tub's front chamfer corners and
  sweeping forward and slightly inward to the drum axle. Tall at the front (they rise above the drum top like horns,
  rough/torn top edges in aws74) and lower at the root.
- Each carries a bearing (silver ball bearing with a shaft end visible in the robot's left arm, aws74/aws76) at the drum
  axle. The robot's right arm also has a small square boss.
- Held: bolted to the chassis corners (aws77: three small screw heads near the right arm root/bearing).

## Drum (weapon)
- Horizontal axis across the robot (x), between the arms, centred, its axis about at the tub mid-height; the drum
  bottom clears the floor by a few mm.
- Black printed cylinder about 22-24 mm diameter and about 40-45 mm long (about 45 % of tub width), on the robot's
  left/centre; on the robot's right end a shorter bright aluminium cylinder (about 18 mm diameter, about 12-15 mm long,
  with small holes/screws): the outrunner motor bell (motor-in-arm drive, bell spins with the drum).
- Two silver metal **hook teeth**: crescent plates about 2 mm thick wrapped round the drum, one at each end of the
  black body, 180 deg apart; each rises from the drum surface to a hooked tip about 4-5 mm proud.
- Mechanism: brushless outrunner fixed to the right arm, its bell bolted to the drum; the drum turns on the motor's
  bearings on the right and the arm bearing on the left.

## Spikes / forks (2)
- Very thin flat blades (about 1-1.5 mm), long isosceles triangles seen from the side (about 8-10 mm tall at the root,
  needle tip), set vertically (thin edge up in plan), one under each arm, just outboard of the drum ends.
- Root clamped to the arm's outer lower face (screws in aws77), they slope down and forward, tips at or just above the
  floor, reaching about 40-45 mm ahead of the drum.

## What is not visible
- Battery, ESCs, receiver: hidden under the plate. Charge/link: none visible beside the switch.
