# Thwack Attack: reference photo descriptions

Source photos: `reference/thwack_attack.aws74.webp`, `thwack_attack.aws76.webp`, `thwack_attack.aws77.webp` (AWS wiki, cut out; the green tint on metal edges is cut-out halo).

Robot type: **overhead axe/hammer** robot. A long black **hammer arm** is pivoted at a blue **gearbox tower** at the front-centre. At rest it lies back over the top of the robot, and its axe head sits behind the rear. The body is a 4-wheel box with an **aluminium front wedge plate** and **two long forks** below it.

Conventions: **front** = the wedge/forks end. **W** = body width across the outer faces of the orange side plates. Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

---

## Main parts (consistent across all photos)
1. **Inner frame**: **blue (and purple) printed** chassis tub, mostly hidden. It shows at the front, under the top plates and around the axles.
2. **Top plates (×2)**: flat **salmon/light-orange printed** rectangular plates, each about 0.4 W wide × 0.75 W long × 0.03 W thick. There is one on the left and one on the right of a **central longitudinal channel** about 0.15 W wide, where the arm lies. Each plate has **4 silver Phillips screws**, roughly at the corners. The right plate carries a **white "ON / OFF" label** and a **small rectangular slide-switch hole**, at its front-right in AWS76/77 and rear in AWS74. The plates also differ in length: the arm tower sits in a cut-out of the left/front plate.
3. **Side plates (×2)**: **bright orange-red** printed **stadium-shaped (rounded-rectangle) plates**, about 0.04 W thick, 1.0 W long and 0.3 W tall. They are mounted vertically on the outside of each side, covering the outer faces of both wheels on that side. Each has **2 silver Phillips screws** at mid-height (into the axle stand-offs or frame), and the left plate in AWS76 has a small hole between them. A raised plate rim covers the wheel ends. The side plates are slightly taller than the wheels, so the robot runs on the wheels' exposed lower arcs. The wheels protrude below the side plates by only about 0.02 W *(guess)*.
4. **Wheels (×4)**: two per side, between the frame and the side plates, at the front and rear ends of each side plate. Each is Ø about 0.27 W and 0.12 W wide. They have a **grey/lilac knurled printed TPU tyre** on a **purple hub**. A **black rubber band / O-ring belt** runs over the top of the front and rear wheel on each side (visible as a black line along the inside top edge of the side plate), likely coupling the wheels, so each side is belt-linked 2WD/4WD *(guess)*. Purple stand-off pins/axle bosses sit between the wheels. One drive motor per side is presumed in the blue frame *(guess)*.
5. **Hammer gearbox tower**: a **blue printed** housing at the **front-centre-left**, just behind the wedge. It is roughly a "D"/tombstone shape in side view: about 0.12 W thick, 0.3 W tall above the plates and 0.3 W long. It sits in the left side of the central channel. Through a slot in its rounded top you can see **black gear teeth** (a large spur gear or gear sector). There is a **silver screw at the axle centre** on its outer face (the pivot axle end). A second blue cheek plate on the other side holds the pivot shaft. A short black wire loop runs from the tower over to the right top plate (a motor lead, AWS77).
6. **Hammer arm**: a **black printed (or carbon) arm**, about 0.06 W wide × 0.06 W tall and about 1.1–1.3 W long from pivot to head. Its root is on the gearbox pivot at the front. It runs back down the central channel, **angled slightly upward**, and extends **past the rear** of the robot by about 0.5 W. It has an irregular/scuffed top edge.
7. **Hammer head (axe)**: at the arm's free end, a **round black hub** about 0.3 W in diameter. It looks like a **small brushless outrunner or a round printed boss** with **4–5 black socket screws** around a centre pair (rosette). It is sandwiched between **two dark steel/green-tinted plates**. The plate outline is a **curved sickle/hook blade**: one long curved **spike rising up** (about 0.35 W above the hub) and one **pointed toe extending forward-down** below the hub (about 0.3 W). Overall the head is about 0.55 W tall. The blade plates are about 0.015 W thick and are bolted through the hub. *(The hub may be a reinforcing printed boss or a mass. It is probably not a spinning disc, since the blade shape is fixed relative to the arm; guess.)*
8. **Front wedge plate**: a **scratched aluminium/steel plate** spanning the full body width (about 1.0 W) and about 0.3 W tall. It is angled forward at about 60–70° from horizontal, a steep wedge/bulldozer blade, with its lower edge near the floor. It is fixed by **two columns of 3 silver Phillips screws** (about 6 total) into the blue frame. The right edge has a small **cut/chamfer**.
9. **Forks (×2)**: two **long narrow steel strips** (about 0.04 W wide, 0.015 W thick), on edge or flat, pass **under the wedge plate** near its left and right quarters (spacing about 0.5 W). They stick forward about 0.8–0.9 W ahead of the wedge. Each ends in an **arrowhead/barbed tip**: a widened step about 0.08 W tall, then a tapered point. They are green-tinted and silver-edged (metal), lying at floor level. They are fixed underneath the frame *(guess: screwed to the frame floor)*.

## Mechanism (inferred)
- The gearbox (a motor plus spur-gear reduction, with gear teeth visible in the blue tower) swings the arm **about a transverse axle** at the front-centre. At rest the arm lies back along the top, with the head behind the robot. Firing swings the arm up over the top and down **forward** so the axe tip strikes in front, over the forks: a classic **overhead "thwack" hammer**. The forks and wedge hold the opponent in the strike zone.
