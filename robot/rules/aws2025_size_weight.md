# AWS 2025 size and weight (source of `profile.aws2025`)

Checked 2026-11-12 from the Bristol Bot Builders summary of the AWS 2025 rules (ruleset updated 19/10/25):
https://bristolbotbuilders.com/guides/ and https://bristolbotbuilders.com/aws/

- 2a Weight: "Roller: 150g, Non-Roller (shufflers, walkers, hovercrafts): 225g"
- 3a Size: "Robots of all weights must sit entirely within a square floor space of 150mm, with no overhang allowed at any height."
- No height limit is stated.

Old rule (AWS v4.3, https://robotwars101.org/ants/rules.htm), now superseded: fit inside a 4 in (101.6 mm) cube; may expand
only after the fight starts and only by remote control. Expansion/cluster wording in the 2025 PDF not yet read (PDF text
not extractable by fetch); verify before adding an expanded-state check.
